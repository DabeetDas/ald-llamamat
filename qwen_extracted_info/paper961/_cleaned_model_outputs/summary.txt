  "evidence": "This study addresses the problem of patterning-induced degradations to organic light-emitting diodes (OLEDs) by using a thin (10 Å) atomic-layer-deposited (ALD) Al2O3 film as both an electron-injection layer and a protecting layer for the electroluminescent material, poly[1-methoxy-4-(2'-ethyl-hexyloxy)-2,5-phenylene vinylene] (MEH-PPV). The ALD Al2O3 film was applied using trimethylaluminum (TMA) and H2O as precursors at 75 °C. The performance of the photo-patterned OLEDs was further improved by using a high-conductivity hole-injection layer, which increased accumulation of holes at the EL–buffer interface to enhance electron injection."
