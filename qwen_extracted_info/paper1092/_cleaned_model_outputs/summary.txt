  "evidence": "A thin ﬁlm of hafnium dioxide (HfO 2) was formed on the surface of Si(100) by atomic layer deposition (ALD) using Hf(O-iPr) 4 as an Hf source and O 2 as an oxidant. The deposited ﬁlm consisted of a monoclinic crystal phase included in an amorphous matrix, which was confirmed by X-ray diffraction. The film showed an equivalent-oxide thickness (EOT) of 2.1 nm and a leakage current density of 8.9 × 10 − 6 A/cm2 at − 1 V."
