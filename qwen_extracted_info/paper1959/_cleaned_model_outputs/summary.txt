  "evidence": "Atomic layer epitaxy of InAs is demonstrated. Saturation of the growth rate of one monolayer per cycle is achieved at a growth temperature of 340°C. Trimethylindium (TMIn) and 10% AsH3 in H2 were used as In and As source materials, respectively. The ALE growth sequence consisted of (1) TMIn exposure, (2) H2 purging, (3) AsH3 exposure, and (4) H2 purging."
