The SiO2 film thicknesses deposited during one silanol exposure were dependent on the substrate temperature, silanol pressure, and silanol exposure time. Likewise, the lower temperatures reduce the cross-linking rates between the siloxane polymers that self-limit the SiO2 deposition. The study also highlights the role of pyridine derivatives in catalyzing both nucleation and cross-linking reactions during rapid SiO2 ALD."
