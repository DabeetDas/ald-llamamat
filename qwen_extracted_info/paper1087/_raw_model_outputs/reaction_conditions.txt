These growth rates with Si2Cl6 are higher than that with SiH2Cl2, and are obtained with reactant exposures lower than those of the SiH2Cl2 case. At process temperature of 573 °C where the wafer temperature during Si2Cl6 pulse is 513 °C, the growth rate increased with Si2Cl6 exposure owing to thermal deposition of Si2Cl6. The deposited films are nonstoichiometric SiN, and were easily oxidized by air exposure to contain 7 –8 at.% of oxygen in the bulk film."
