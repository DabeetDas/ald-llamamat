The effects of substrate temperature on film growth, structure, and properties are analyzed. The films exhibit amorphous-to-crystalline transitions, and the growth rate and film characteristics are influenced by the temperature range. The optimal growth rate is achieved in the intermediate temperature range (225–275°C), while high temperatures lead to decomposition of precursors and rougher film surfaces."
