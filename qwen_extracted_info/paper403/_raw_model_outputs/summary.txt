  "evidence": "Atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) of Si has been demonstrated by using remote He plasma low energy ion bombardment to desorb H from an H-passivated Si(100) surface at low temperatures and subsequently chemisorbing Si2H6 on the surface in a self-limiting fashion. It was found that as He pressures and r.f. powers are raised the plasma potential and mean free paths are reduced, leading to lower He bombardment energies but higher fluxes. Optimal He bombardment parameters were determined to be 30 W at 100 mTorr process pressure at 400 °C for 1-3 min. The growth per cycle saturates with long Si2H6 dosing at a level which increases slightly with He bombardment time. It was also confirmed that the growth is achieved only by using alternate He bombardment and Si2H6 dosing."
