 "evidence": "Film composition and depth profiling were studied by medium energy ion scattering (MEIS). Compositional depth profiling and low-level residual impurity detection were performed by time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectroscopy (TOF-SIMS). Film thickness was also monitored by single-wavelength ellipsometry at 635 nm. Film morphology was examined by high-resolution electron microscopy (HRTEM). X-ray diffraction (XRD) experiments were carried out at the Brookhaven National Laboratory, National Synchrotron Light Source, Beamline X20C in order to understand crystalline structure of as-deposited films and to monitor (in real-time) crystallization and phase transformation upon in-situ annealing."
