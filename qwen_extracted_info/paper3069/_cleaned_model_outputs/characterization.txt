The residual carbon content in the films was measured by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). The uniformity of PZT films deposited on the 3D structure was examined by transmission electron microscopy (TEM) or scanning TEM (STEM). Piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM) was performed to probe the ferroelectric polarization in crystallized PZT films. The composition values in the figure are average values of the local EDS measurements. The film thickness of the PZT was within a range of 50–51 nm. The relative intensity among the various cations was constant. The PZT film deposited using Ti/H20849Oi–Pr/H208502/H20849DPM/H208502 had 3.1% carbon, while the PZT films deposited using TTIP contained a higher amount of carbon, 15.6%. The PZT film deposited on the substrate was uniform, both in film thickness and cation composition. The PZT film was amorphous. The PFM measurement gave a clear contrast image for the piezoresponse."
