The self-regulated growth is achieved through the chemisorption of precursor molecules onto predeposited cation layers, with the catalytic decomposition of Ti/H20849Oi–Pr/H208504 contributing to the nonsaturating growth behavior of Ti. The catalytic decomposition of the TTIP precursor on the growing film surface induced the nonsaturated growth rate of Ti."
