  "evidence": "The main leakage current mechanism is governed by the Poole–Frenkel conduction mechanism. The leakage current dependence on the ZrO2 layer thickness was investigated. Figure 3 shows the J–V curves of ZrO2 MIM capacitors grown at 275◦C with a thickness of 6 nm (a ), 8 nm (b ) and 10 nm (c ). The 6 nm thick samples show higher leakage current compared with the 8 and 10 nm samples. All the insets in figure 3 show Poole–Frenkel (P–F) plots of the leakage current data: the linear relationship of (J/E) in log scale versus E1/2 in figures 3(a) and (b ) demonstrates that the leakage current for the 6 and 8 nm samples is mainly dominated by the P–F mechanism. The thicker 10 nm sample shows strong temperature dependent leakage current under the condition of E< 1000 (V/cm)1/2, while at a higher field reveals the P–F dominant conduction mechanism. It is believed that trap assisted tunnelling current occurs in the lower field with a weak temperature dependence, as shown in the inset of figure 3(c), while the P–F emission is the main mechanism at higher fields due to the field enhanced thermal excitation of trapped electrons in the high-k dielectric materials."
