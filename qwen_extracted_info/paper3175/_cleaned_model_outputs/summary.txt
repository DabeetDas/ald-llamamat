  "evidence": "MgO thin films have been grown on Si(100) using bis(cyclopentadienyl)magnesium and water as precursors. The thermal stability of an ALD-MgO film as a function of annealing temperature has been first investigated by XPS. The MgO films were annealed at 600, 800, and 900°C under N2 ambient. XPS results indicate that the as-deposited MgO film is quite reactive to atmospheric moisture and carbon dioxide and has a sharp interface with the substrate. The spectra of samples annealed at 600°C show that the Mg–OH groups have been turned into MgO. RTA also induces the formation of magnesium silicate, whose content increases with increasing annealing temperature."
