  "evidence": "Various physical methods have been used to characterize the quality and structure of the Zn S thin films. These include XRD, ellipsometry, RBS and Auger spectroscopies, SEM, and TEM. Differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) experiments were carried out at the Applications Laboratory of Seiko Instruments Inc. in Tokyo, Japan. The DSC instrument capable of ultrahigh sensitivity measurements was the Seiko DSC 120. The ETA technique has already been applied to the study of recrystallization, polymorphic transitions, solid state reactions, etc. The DSC curve exhibits a distinct exothermic peak centered at 478 °C. The temperature dependence of the radon diffusion coefficient can be expressed by the Arrhenius law. The stepwise heating experiments confirmed the features described above. X-ray diffraction and ellipsometric experiments have shown that the ALE-grown Zn S thin films are, after an initial, poorly ordered 50-100 nm layer, stoichiometric and highly textured."
