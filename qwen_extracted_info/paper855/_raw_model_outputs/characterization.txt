  "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, Kratos, Axis Ultra DLD) measurements were used to investigate the initial growth regime and the composition of YMO thin films. A field emission scanning transmission electron microscope (FEI, Titon S/TEM) was used to image the interface of YMO and the substrate with an accelerating voltage 200 kV. High resolution transmission electron microscope (HR-TEM) images were collected using an ultrascan, 2×2k digital camera (Gatan) with digital micrograph acquisition capabilities. The crystal structures of the deposited films were characterized using X-ray diffraction (XRD) (Panalytical X’Pert) equipped with a Cu K α radiation source (λ = 1.54 Å). The Mn K-edge was measured at Stanford Synchrotron Radiation Laboratory (SSRL) on beamline 4-3. Magnetic properties were carried out using a superconducting quantum interference device (SQUID, Quantum Design MPMS). The temperature (T) dependence of the DC magnetic susceptibility (χ) (χ vs. T) was measured under zero-field-cooled (ZFC) and field-cooled (FC) conditions with a magnetic field of 100 Oe in the temperature range from 5 K to 298 K. The growth rate was determined by an ellipsometer (J.A. Woollam Co., M-88) and high resolution SEM cross section analysis (FEI, Nova 600)."
