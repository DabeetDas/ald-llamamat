  "evidence": "AlN thin films were grown by an alternate supply of trimethyl aluminum (TMA) and ammonia, i.e., according to the processing scheme of the atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) technique. The temperature region where uniform films were obtained was 325–425 °C, which is remarkably low compared with most of the CVD processes employing the same precursors. The films exhibited good crystallinity. Cross-sectional SEM studies indicated that the films consisted of densely packed grains and had rather smooth surfaces. With increasing deposition temperature the refractive index increased from 1.97 to 2.25, which was attributed to densification of the film structure."
