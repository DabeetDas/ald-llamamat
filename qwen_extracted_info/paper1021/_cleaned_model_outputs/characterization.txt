  "evidence": "Quantitative information on micro-structure of the sample surfaces was drawn from atomic force microscopy (AFM) investigations and from high-resolution scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images. The optical properties of the films were characterized with photoluminescence (PL), micro-PL and cathodoluminescence (CL). PL studies were performed over a temperature range of approximately 2–300 K. The PL emission at 2.7903 eV at 2 K is most likely to be due to acceptor bound exciton (ABE) PL. The 2. 25 eV emission was related to Cu complexes acting as deep acceptors, whereas the 2.02 eV emission was assigned to so-called ‘self-activated emission’, with a deep acceptor center due to a zinc vacancy complex.
