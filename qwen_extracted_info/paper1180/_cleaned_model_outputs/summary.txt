  "evidence": "The high vapor pressure, thermal stability, and relatively small ligands of the precursor facilitate efficient ALD. Typical self-limiting growth and an ALD temperature window of 220 −260 °C are observed along with significantly high growth per cycle (GPC) ( ∼1.7 Å) and short incubation cycles ( ∼6) at 220 °C. The as-grown polycrystalline films (average grain size ∼20 nm and negligible impurities) were evident from plan-view transmission electron microscopy. The variation in film resistivity with increasing film thickness and deposition temperature was investigated with and without annealing. Films deposited at 260 °C show low resistivity ( ∼12.9 μΩ cm), which further decreases ( ∼9.8 μΩ cm) postannealing at 500 °C. A thin Ru film is successfully deposited with 100% step-coverage on a dual-trench structure having an aspect ratio of ∼6.3 (minimum width: ∼15 nm)."
