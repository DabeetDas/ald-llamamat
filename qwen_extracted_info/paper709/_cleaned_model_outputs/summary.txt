  "evidence": "A dependence of structural properties of TiO2 films grown on both Si- and Ti-substrates by atomic layer deposition (ALD) at the temperature range of 250–300 ◦C from titanium ethoxide and water on the number of reaction cycles N was investigated... The average growth per cycle of TiO2 deposited from titanium methoxide and water (0.052 ± 0.01 nm/cycle) was essentially higher than that for TiO2 grown from titanium ethoxide and water (0.043 ± 0.01 nm/cycle). Ligands of smaller sizes were found to promote the higher crystallinity of TiO2 in comparison with the case of using the titanium precursor with ligands of bigger sizes."
