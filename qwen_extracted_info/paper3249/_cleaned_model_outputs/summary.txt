  "evidence": "PECVD and PEALD of ruthenium films using RuEtcp2 as a precursor and N2/H2/Ar plasma as a reducing agent were characterized. A self-adjusting process to overcome the previously reported inhibition of Ru PEALD on TaN substrates was investigated. The physical properties of PECVD/PEALD Ru films were compared to characteristics of sputtered Ru films within the categories resistivity, impurities, crystal structure, conformity and Cu plating. The results show that Ru(-C) films produced by PECVD and PEALD have high conductivity and good conformity, comparable to PVD Ru. The presence of carbon impurities was observed, and the deposition process was optimized to overcome inhibition issues on TaN substrates. The study also highlights the importance of process parameters such as temperature, plasma power, and gas flow in achieving optimal film properties."
