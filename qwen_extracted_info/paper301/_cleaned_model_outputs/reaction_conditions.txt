  "evidence": "The competition between the driving force from bulk Gibbs free energy and the barrier due to the 'pinning effect' of Al2O3 sub-layer determines the crystallization behavior. The crystallization occurs in the HfO2 sub-layers, but not in the Al2O3 sub-layers. The HfO2 sub-layers are inclined to initiate the crystallization, while the Al2O3 sub-layers tend to impede the growth of the crystalline phase. The crystallization temperature is influenced by the thickness of the HfO2 sub-layers and the stacking sequence per supercycle. The Hf–Al bond breaks and reforms Hf–O–Al bond. The intensity of Al–O binding energy increases and that of Hf–Al–O binding energy decreases after annealing. The Al–O, Hf–O–Al, and Hf–O binding states are identified through XPS analysis."
