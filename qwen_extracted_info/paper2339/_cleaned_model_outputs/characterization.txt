  "evidence": "The O/In molar ratio in the In2O3 films was evaluated from elemental depth profiles generated using radio frequency glow discharge optical emission spectroscopy (rf-GDOES; Horiba JY-5000RF) at an applied power of 35 W and an Ar pressure of 600 Pa. Hydrogen was analyzed by high-resolution elastic recoil detection analysis (HR-ERDA) using the same apparatus. Impurities in the In2O3 films were analyzed by time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry (TOF-SIMS; Ulvac-Phi TRIFT-II) within a 20 ×20 µm area inside a 100 ×100 µm crater etched by a Ga+ ion beam at an acceleration voltage of 15 kV. The thickness of the In2O3 films was determined using a spectroscopic ellipsometer (J.A. Woolam, M-2000U) in conjunction with the Cauchy dispersion function."
