 "evidence": "The center thickness and refractive index of the Al2O3 films were monitored in situ by spectroscopic ellipsometry measurements. The Al, O, and C-content of the film were determined by Rutherford backscattering spectrometry. Elastic recoil detection was used to determine the H-content. High-resolution scanning electron microscopy images using a FEI Nova NanoSEM 600 electron microscope revealed the conformality. The dielectric constant and breakdown voltage of these test structures were determined by 10 kHz capacitance-voltage and current-voltage measurements with an HP4275A multifrequency LCR meter and an Agilent 4155B parameter analyzer."
