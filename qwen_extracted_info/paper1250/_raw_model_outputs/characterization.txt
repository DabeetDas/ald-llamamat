  "evidence": "Crystallographic nature of as-deposited films is studied by X-ray Diffraction (XRD), high resolution transmission electron microscopy (HR-TEM) imaging and selected area electron diffraction (SAED). XRD patterns are obtained with Rigaku Smartlab X-ray Diffractometer, equipped with Cu-Kα source while HR-TEM images and SAED patterns are acquired with Tecnai G2 model F30 TEM from FEI at beam energy of 300 kV. The chemical composition of the films is studied with X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) with Electron Spectroscopy for Chemical Analysis (ESCA) of model AXIS Supra by Kratos Analytical, UK (SHIMADZU group). Optical transmission measurements are carried out with Perkin-Elmer Lambda 950 UV-NIR spectrophotometer with an integrated sphere assembly. Kelvin probe or surface photovoltage measurements are carried out with a commercially available Kelvin probe (from Besocke, Delta Phi) with a sensitivity of ca. 1 mV. The thermogravimetric/differential thermal analysis (TG/DTA) is used to study the thermal properties of the SDMDTC precursor. The in-situ quartz crystal microbalance (QCM) studies are carried out to understand the growth mechanism of Sb2Se3 ALD at 150°C. The combination of diffraction and imaging tools are emphasized to confirm the structural properties of the as-deposited Sb2Se3 at 150°C."
