  "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) analysis revealed no significant reduction of arsenic oxides upon deposition of HfO2 on GaAs using tetrakis(dimethyl-amino)hafnium as the metallic precursor. High-resolution transmission electron microcopy analysis verified a smooth interface between HfO2 and sulfur-passivated GaAs. The XPS results also indicate the existence of Sulfur at the interface, primarily bonded to Ga atoms. The abrupt transition of high-frequency C-V curve from accumulation to depletion implies relatively good interface quality. The room temperature leakage current density–voltage (J-V) curve of a MOS capacitor with a 115 Å thick HfO2 on the NH42S-treated sample shows low leakage current density. The linear dependency of capacitance equivalent thickness (CET) on HfO2 physical thickness was evaluated from the accumulation capacitance from 1 MHz high-frequency C-V curves. The bidirectional 10 kHz C-V sweeps reveal a hysteresis of 500 mV for the annealed sample at 500 °C in N2, as shown in the inset of Fig. 4(a).
