  "evidence": "PEALD produces self-limiting growth by allowing both reagents to individually react with the surface until saturation is achieved. Two conditions must be met to ensure self-limiting behavior in pulsed PECVD. First, the precursor must be inert with dry O2, so that no deposition occurs during the plasma off step. Second, the precursor is diluted with excess O2 so that no deposition is observed under continuous wave (CW) plasma operation. Instead of relying on self-limiting surface chemistry, growth terminates during each plasma on step due to complete consumption of the precursor. Under CW operation the TiCl4 is either completely oxidized in the gas-phase or lost through sacrificial deposition upstream of the substrate. Deposition is then readily achieved when the plasma is pulsed at low frequency ( ∼Hz). Under proper operation, the deposition rate per pulse is independent of both plasma on and off times, enabling digital control over film thickness. Deposition proceeds through a combination of ALD-like surface growth as well as a PECVD-like contribution that occurs at the initiation of each plasma pulse."
