  "evidence": "Atomic layer deposition (ALD) of TiO2 on SiO2 powder using sequential addition of TiCl4 and H2O vapors has been investigated by infrared spectroscopy. In the first cycle, TiCl4 reacts monofunctionally or bifunctionally with surface silanols forming (Si-O-)nTi-Cl4-n (n = 1, 2) species. Subsequent addition of water vapor leads to the hydrolysis of the (Si-O-)nTi-Cl4-n to form a Ti-O-Ti network, and at the same time, some cleavage of Si-O-Ti bonds occurs, regenerating Si-OH in the process."
