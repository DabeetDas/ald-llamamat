  "evidence": "The EOT and VFB were calculated from the analytical quantum mechanical model. The structures of the TaSi2.5/HfO2/Si were characterized by cross-sectional transmission electron microscopy (TEM) analyses. The impurities such as carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen of the HfO2 gate dielectrics were investigated by secondary ion mass spectrometry (SIMS) analysis with a 1 keV cesium primary ion (Cs+) beam. The thickness and uniformity of the HfO2 films were measured with an ellipsometer over 300 mm wafers. Capacitance–voltage (C–V) and current–voltage (I–V) measurements were performed in parallel mode on an HP 4284A LCR meter at high frequency (100 kHz) and an HP 4156C parameter analyzer."
