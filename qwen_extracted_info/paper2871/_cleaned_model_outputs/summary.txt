Hafnium silicate films with /C24 80% HfO2 were amorphous up to 700 /C14 C. The hafnium silicate films deposited at 220 /C14 C have an average dielectric constant of 9.8 with a flatband voltage (Vfb) and a hysteresis voltage in capacitance–voltage (C–V) measurements of 0 V and less than 0.18 V, respectively."
