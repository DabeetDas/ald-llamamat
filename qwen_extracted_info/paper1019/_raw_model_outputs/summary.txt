  "evidence": "The ALD (and CVD) processes must involve as a critical step the adsorption and reaction of precursors or precursor fragments on the substrate surface. For this reason, there is a considerable interest in using surface science methodology to explore the reaction pathways of precursor molecules on single crystalline surfaces under controlled conditions. The temperatures for successful ALD of HfO2 from HfI4 and O2 lie within the range 770–1020 K. The results indicate that the metallic Hf formed at 870 K is in the form of particles. Oxidation of this film by O2 at low pressure does not result in complete Hf oxidation. This suggests that complete oxidation of Hf is a critical step when using HfI4 as precursor in atomic layer deposition."
