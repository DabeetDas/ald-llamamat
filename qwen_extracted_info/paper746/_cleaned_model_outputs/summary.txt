  "evidence": "Thin Ruthenium (Ru) films were deposited on native SiO2 on Si substrate by thermal atomic layer deposition (ALD) using dicarbonyl-bis(5-methyl-2,4-hexanediketonato)Ru(II) precursor with O2 and H2O as reactant. Through density functional theory (DFT) calculations, it is shown that adsorption of the Ru precursor would spontaneously occur on metallic Ru surface. Ru ALD using H2O reactant is performed on SiO2 powder to successfully form Ru@SiO2 core-shell structure."
