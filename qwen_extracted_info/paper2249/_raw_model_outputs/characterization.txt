  "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectra and electron microscopy indicate that in situ desorption of a protective As layer on the In0.53Ga0.47As surface followed by ALD of Al2O3 produced an atomically abrupt interface without Fermi level pinning. The C-V measurements were performed in a shielded probe station. The stage temperature was increased to 270 °C for Al2O3 deposition. Ex situ XPS measurements were performed to detect any changes in bonding at the Al2O3 / In0.53Ga0.47As interface after 10 cycles of ALD. Cross-section transmission electron microscopy (TEM) samples were prepared by standard techniques including mechanical polishing using diamond lapping films followed by 2.8–0.8 kV argon ion milling. High resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM) and atomic-number sensitive high-angle annular dark-field (HAADF) imaging in scanning TEM were performed. The C-V measurements were performed using a HP4284A precision LCR meter. I-V measurements were made using a Keithley 230 programmable voltage source and a Keithley 6512 programmable electrometer."
