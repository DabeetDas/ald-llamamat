  "evidence": "The presence and amount of Zn deposited on the wings was confirmed by X-ray fluorescence (XRF) measurements with a Philips PW 1480 WDS equipment using Rh-excitation. The scanning electron microscopy (SEM) imaging of the cicada wings was performed with a LEO DSM 982 Gemini scanning electron microscope using 2 kV acceleration voltage. Film reflectance and transmittance spectra were recorded with a Hitachi U-2000 UV/vis-spectrophotometer. Film thicknesses were determined from the reflectance spectra on Si(100) substrates by a spectrophotometric modeling method presented by Ylilammi and Ranta-Aho. Water contact angles on cicada wings and on soda lime glass reference substrates were measured by a CAM 200 contact angle measurement system (KSV Instruments)."
