  "evidence": "The XPS data of the HfO films grown with the different O3 concentration and PDA conditions, which were reported earlier, 5 were analyzed again based on the findings from the TOF-SIMS data. The film thickness was measured by ellipsometry and cross-section high-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM). The interface structure of the gate dielectric films was investigated by XPS and TOF-SIMS. The stoichiometry ratio (O/Hf) of the ALD HfO2 films was measured by Rutherford backscattering spectroscopy (RBS). Capacitance-voltage (C-V) measurements for the capacitance equivalent thickness (CET) estimation were performed. The interfacial trap density Dit was measured by the conductance method. Auger electron spectroscopy (AES) confirmed the SIMS results."
