  "evidence": "The ALD and the tellurization annealing of Ge –Sb films were performed in the same reactor without air exposure. The tellurization annealing was performed by repeating tBu2Te supply and purging at a constant temperature. The supply time of tBu2Te was 5 s and the purging time was 60 s. The temperatures of the container and the delivery line of tBu2Te were 30 /C14C and 40 /C14C, respectively. Since Ge –Sb and GST films are readily oxidized in air, we passivated the ALD Ge –Sb films and the tellurized films by approximately 4 nm-thick silicon oxide thin film using an e-beam evaporator immediately after unloading from the reactor."
