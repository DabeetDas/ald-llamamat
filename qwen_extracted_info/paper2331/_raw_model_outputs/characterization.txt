  "evidence": "The thicknesses and the grain sizes of MoS2 films were measured by atomic force microscopy (AFM) in the contact mode. Scanning electron microscopy (SEM), Raman spectroscopy, and high-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM) were used to observe their nanostructures, which showed that the film possessed a complete crystal structure. To obtain the cross-sectional SEM image, the grown MoS2 film was deposited with an Al coating by magnetron sputtering and etched out a cross-section by a focused ion beam (FIB). Raman spectroscopy was carried out using a 532 nm laser with a laser power of 0.6 mW. The HRTEM image was taken at an accelerating voltage of 200 kV. To carry out TEM, the grown MoS2 film was exfoliated from the Si substrate and transferred onto a copper TEM grid. The size-dependent piezoelectricity of the grown MoS2 film was measured by PFM with a conductive tip."
