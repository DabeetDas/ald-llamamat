  "evidence": "The ALE growth of A1GaP and GaP films on Si substrates proceeds through alternating exposure to TMG/TMA fluxes and PH3 flux, with a pause between exposures. The growth rate saturates slightly above one monolayer per ALE cycle, possibly due to cross-diffusion of PH3 toward column III at high V/III ratios. The surface diffusion lengths are controlled by substrate temperature and exposure/pause times, promoting 2-D growth. The pronounced defects observed in all the ALE-grown GaP, A1GaP, and GaAs films were planar defects such as stacking faults and microtwins, as well as threading dislocations."
