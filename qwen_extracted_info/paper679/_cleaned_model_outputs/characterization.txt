 "evidence": "The composition of the ﬁlms grown on silicon substrates was examined by X-ray photon spectroscopy(XPS, VG MT 500/1, VG Instrument). X-ray diffractometer(XRD, D5005, Bruker-AXS) using Co Ka radiation was used to investigate the crystalline phase of the ﬁlms. SEM (S-4100, Hitachi) and atomic force microscopy (AFM, Autoprobe M5, Veeco) were also used to investigate the surface morphology. Optical transmittance spectra were taken from the ﬁlms grown on quartz substrates using UV-spectrophotometer (UV 20300, Schmidt Unicam) in wavelength range of 200 –1100 nm. Refractive index was measured by ellipsometry technique (J.A. Woollam Co). The electrical measurements, including current –voltage (HP4140B) and capacitance–voltage (HP4284A) characteristics, were performed on Pt/Ta2O5(10 nm)/Si MOS structure."
