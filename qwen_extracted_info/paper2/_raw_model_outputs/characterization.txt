  "evidence": "The Sn to sulfur ratios of the films were determined by energy dispersive analysis by X-rays (EDAX), X-ray photoeletron spectroscopy (XPS) and electron probe measurements. The tin to S EDAX ratios for the coatings grown at 350–500 °C showed slightly sulfur-rich films SnS 2.04–2.20. XPS showed typically 5 at.-% oxygen contamination on the surface of the films, although variations from ca. 1–10 at.-% were noted dependent in part on the time the film was exposed to air prior to XPS analysis. The bulk XPS chemical shift measurements for films grown at 300–500 °C gave a Sn 3d 5/2 peak at 486.5 –0.1 eV and S 2p 3/2 shift at 161.6–0.2 eV dependent on the sample. Glancing angle X-ray diffraction patterns for films grown from 300–500 °C indicate single phase crystalline SnS 2 is formed. UV meaurements for the films grown at 300–500 °C showed a direct band gap of ca. 2.1 eV. The SEM micrographs showed that at 300 °C the films have an ovoid-like appearance with particles of dimension ca. 500 nm × 100 nm. The film grown at 545 °C was silver-gray in color and analyzed as SnS. XPS showed an elemental ratio of SnS 1.1 and chemical shifts for the Sn d 5/2 peak at 485.7 eV and the S2 p3/2 peak at 161.0 eV. X-ray diffraction revealed orthorhombic SnS and indexed with a = 4.321(4) Å, b = 11.205(4) Å and c = 3.989(4) Å. X-ray powder diffraction patterns were measured on a Philips X-pert diffractometer using unfiltered Cu K a radiation in the reflection mode using glancing incident angle. SEM/EDAX was obtained on a Hitachi S570 instrument using the KEVEX system. Electron microprobe analysis was obtained on a JEOL EMA and referenced against polished sulfur and tin standards. X-ray photoelectron spectra were recorded with a VG ESCALAB 220i XL instrument using focused (300 mm spot) monochromatic Al Ka radiation at a pass energy of 20 eV. Depth profile measurements were obtained by using argon beam sputtering. UV-vis spectra were recorded in the range 200–1000 nm using a Schimadzu double beam instrument, band gaps were calculated by the direct method."
