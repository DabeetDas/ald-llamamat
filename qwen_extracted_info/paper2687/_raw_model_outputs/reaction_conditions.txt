H2O exposures remove Cl from B sites but fail to remove Cl from the Cl-Si species. The initial Cl contamination was due to Cl-Si bond formation at the interface, without Cl incorporation into the oxide film. BCl3/O2 precursors exhibit negligible B2O3 growth at room temperature. At 650 K, B2O3 growth was observed with a decreasing growth rate per BCl3/O2 cycle, corresponding to an increase in the Cl:B atomic ratio. The data in Figs. 2 and 3 show that Si exposure to BCl3 under these conditions is self-limiting, yielding Cl 2p features associated with both B-Cl and Si-Cl chemical bonds. The B2O3 deposition parameters were set at 250 mTorr/360 s for BCl3 precursor, and 350 mTorr/180 s for H2O precursor at room temperature.
