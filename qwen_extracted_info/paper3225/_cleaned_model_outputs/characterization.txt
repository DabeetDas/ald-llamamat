  "evidence": "Energy dispersive X-ray spectrometry (EDX) was applied for the measurements of the aluminum to zirconium cation ratio, and also for the estimation of the film thicknesses, using a Hitachi S-4800 scanning electron microscope (SEM) equipped with an Oxford INCA 350 EDX spectrometer. The PANalytical X’pert Pro MPD diffractometer was used to measure the grazing incidence X-ray diffraction patterns. High-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM) studies for the characterization of the cross-sections of the nanolaminate structures were carried out using FEI Titan Themis 200 instrument. Magnetic measurements on selected samples were performed using vibrating sample magnetometer (VSM) option of the Physical Property Measurement System (PPMS). The charge polarization versus applied voltage loops were measured using Al/Ti/ZrO2-Al2O3/TiN/Si/Al capacitor stacks. Capacitance and conductance measurements were performed against bias voltage applied on Al/Ti/ZrO2(:Al2O3)/TiN/Ti/Si/Al metal-insulator-metal type structures. Admittance parameters were recorded after the forming cycle."
