  "evidence": "In the present work, WN barrier films were made by ALD using bis(tert-butylimido)bis(dimethylamido)tungsten, tBuN)2(Me2N)2W, and ammonia at low substrate temperatures (250– 350 °C). The resistivity of typical ALD tungsten nitride coating is about 1.5×10−3 – 4.0×10−3 V cm. The films were amorphous as deposited. 100% step coverage was obtained inside holes with aspect ratios greater than 40:1. WN films as thin as 1.5 nm proved to be good barriers to diffusion of copper for temperatures up to 600 °C. Annealing for 30 min at temperatures above 725 °C converted the WN to pure, polycrystalline tungsten metal."
