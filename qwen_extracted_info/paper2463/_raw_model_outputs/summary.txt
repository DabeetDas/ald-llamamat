The temperature dependence of the amount of Si adatoms delivered to a Ge(100) surface exhibits bistable self-limiting features. Two plateau regimes are observed: (I) below 260 °C, and (II) between 430 and 500 °C. The Cl coverage depends little on the gas exposure temperature. The study also highlights the role of photon-stimulated desorption in maintaining the self-limiting condition during the growth process."
