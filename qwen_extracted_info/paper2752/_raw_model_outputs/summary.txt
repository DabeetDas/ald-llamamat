  "evidence": "The growth of vanadium dioxide (VO2) thin films using tetrakis (ethyl-methyl) amino vanadium (TEMA V) and H2O by atomic layer deposition (ALD) has been investigated as a function of the exposure dose and residence time. The ALD deposition temperature is low, and the as-deposited VO2 thin films are amorphous, requiring postdeposition annealing to produce polycrystalline films.
