  "evidence": "Thin film thicknesses, and the growth rates derived from them, were determined by X-ray reflectivity (XRR) measurements using a Bruker D8 Advance X-ray diffractometer. Crystallinity was examined with grazing incidence X-ray diffraction (GIXRD) measurements using a PANalytical X’Pert Pro MPD X-ray diffractometer. For in situ high temperature XRD (HTXRD) measurements, an Anton-Paar HTK1200N oven was used. Scanning electron microscope images were acquired using a Hitachi S-4800 field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM). Film compositions were determined from energy dispersive X-ray (EDX) spectra, measured using an INCA Energy 350 EDX spectrometer connected to the S-4800. The Ga/As ratio was measured by Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS) using a 4.8 MeV He beam and Si-detector located at 169 degrees. Light impurities in the film were determined by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA)"
