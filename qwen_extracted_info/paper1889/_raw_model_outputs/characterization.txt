  "evidence": "The chemical composition and impurity content in the films were examined by Auger electron spectroscopy (AES, VG Scientific Microlab 350). HRTEM was used to examine the crystallinity and interfacial layer changes. The Hf density of the films was measured by X-ray fluorescence spectroscopy (XRF, Spectrace, QuanX). The surface morphology of the films was investigated by atomic force microscopy (AFM, JEOL, JSPM5100). All the film thickness data in this study, except for the data shown in Fig. 6, 7 and 10, were obtained using a spectroscopic ellipsometer (J. A. Woolam, EC-400). The glancing angle X-ray diffraction (GAXRD) measurements were carried out using a Philips X’pert Pro MRD X-ray diffractometer with Cu Kα radiation. The electrical properties of the HfO2 films were investigated with a capacitor structure of Pt (100 nm)/HfO2/Si substrate. A Hewlett-Packard 4194A impedance analyzer and a 4140B picoammeter were used for the capacitance–voltage (C–V) and current density–voltage (J–V) measurements, respectively."
