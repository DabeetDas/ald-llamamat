  "evidence": "XRD analysis of as deposited TiO2 layers shows an amorphous structure without any anatase or rutile phases. Analysis of these films with Raman spectroscopy (488 nm excitation wavelength) reveals some already beginning formation of the anatase phase. Material analysis of the TiO2 layers with auger electron spectroscopy revealed that the Ti(NMe2)4 process provides pure TiO2 layers with carbon content below 0.5 at. %. Al content in the layers was measured by EDX and XPS analysis. The measurement model of TiN was adapted to partially oxidized TiN, which indicates an oxidation of the TiN surface. Additional Auger electron spectroscopy (AES) results on samples with AlTiOx on TaN do not show any oxidation of the TaN surface."
