  "evidence": "The feasibility of plasma enhanced batch ALD of Al doped TiO2 could be demonstrated clearly. Ti(NMe2)4 turned out to be the most suitable precursor tested in this batch reactor. With Ti(NMe2)4 the highest deposition rate per cycle of about 0.7 A˚/cycle and a best within wafer film thickness uniformity below 1% (1,) could be reached. First electrical characterizations on planar test structures demonstrated that a significant reduction of leakage current density is possible. Processing with 350 /C14C ozone PDA, inserting Al2O3 interlayers of about 4 nm and in situ doping of the TiO2 layer with about 4–5 at. % Al is most effective. Moreover, additional leakage current reduction could be achieved by plasma assisted deposition using O3 and O-radicals during the oxidation ALD half cycle. With such an optimized process the leakage current density could be reduced to about 5 /C210/C07 A/cm2 at 2 V. Capacity measurements of planar structures revealed a total k-value of about 35 for 125 nm AlTiOx, including 4 nm Al2O3 interlayer. As a consequence, AlTiOx shows significant improvement over pure TiO2 films due to the much lower leakage current density at same k values. Also, AlTiOx outperforms single Al2O3 layers because of its 5 times higher k value. Thus, not yet reaching the desired leakage current density of below 10 /C07 A/cm2, this material system is very promising for further optimization."
