  "evidence": "Ellipsometry and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) provide information on film thickness and elemental composition, respectively. Atomic force microscopy (AFM) and cross-sectional TEM analysis, on the other hand, provide nanoscale information on the film. A FTA 2000 dynamic contact angle analyzer has been used to measure the water contact angle. The XPS, AFM, and TEM analyses were carried out in the facilities of the Geballe Laboratory for Advanced Materials (GLAM) at Stanford University. The incident angle for the ellipsometer is 70 °. The XPS system used is an SSI S-Probe monochromatized spectrometer, which uses Al (Kα) radiation (1486 eV) as a probe. Surface morphology was investigated by AFM (Digital Instruments Inc., Santa Barbara, CA). The TEM used in this work is a Philips CM20 field emission gun (FEG) microscopy. The operating voltage for this high-resolution TEM is 200 kV, with 2.4 Å point-to-point resolution and 2 Å line resolution."
