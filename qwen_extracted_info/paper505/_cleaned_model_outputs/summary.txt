  "evidence": "We have grown CdTe/MnTe tilted superlattices by atomic layer epitaxy. These heterostructures are formed by depositing, in the step-flow growth mode, fractional monolayer superlattices (CdTe),,(MnTe),, with p = m + n z 1, onto 2’ A and 2’ B Cd0.65Zn0.05Te vicinal substrates. The half-monolayer of CdTe is grown via two atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) cycles, during which 4 monolayer (ML) of Cd, then 1 ML of Te are sent onto the surface. Thanks to this step-flow growth mode, the ALE of CdTe/MnTe-tilted superlattices (TSLs) on vicinal surfaces is possible. The TSLs are grown at temperatures ranging from 240°C to 280°C."
