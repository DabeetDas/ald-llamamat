  "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) demonstrated low carbon and nitrogen contamination. The thickness and crystallinity of the ZrO2 thin films were evaluated by X-ray reflectivity (XRR) and X-ray diffraction (XRD). Cross sections were investigated with a Zeiss NEON 40 EsB CrossBeam system, which allows high-resolution field emission scanning electron microscopy (FE-SEM) imaging. For atomic force microscopy (AFM) studies, a Multimode V instrument was used. The chemical composition of the films was studied by means of X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). Metal−insulator−metal (MIM) structures were used for electrical characterization. The fundamental electrical characterization was performed at room temperature under normal atmosphere. Leakage current characterization was done using a Keithley 2611 source meter. Capacitance −voltage measurements, C(V), were performed with a small oscillating signal of 50 mV amplitude, at a frequency of 100 kHz, by use of a HP 4284A LCR meter. The gas species present in the reactor during the ALD cycle were investigated by use of a Hiden HAL/3F 501 RC quadrupole mass spectrometer (QMS). The mass changes on the substrate were recorded on a Maxtek TM 400 quartz crystal microbalance (QCM)."
