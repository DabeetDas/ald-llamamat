  "evidence": "The formation of AIN thin films with low carbon impurity levels is achieved in UHV using an ALG process at surface temperatures as low as 600 K. At lower deposition temperatures, higher levels of impurities are deposited in the films. These results are in good agreement with our studies of AIN deposition in an atmospheric MOCVD reactor using the same precursors, where a temperature regime of 613 + 40 K was identified for ALG of AIN films."
