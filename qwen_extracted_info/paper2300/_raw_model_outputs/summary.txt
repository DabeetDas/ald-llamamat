  "evidence": "When Si layers were grown on Ge(100) by alternating SiH2Cl2 gas exposure and irradiation with synchrotron radiation, bistable growth occurred at temperatures (i) below 400 °C (0.21 Å/cycle) and (ii) between 470 and 530 °C (0.42 Å/cycle).
