  "evidence": "According to ellipsometric measurements, the thickness of residual oxide layers on the surface of matrices was 1–2 nm. The composition of nanolayers was controlled using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (HP-5950A (Al Kα radiation with = 1486 eV) and SER-1 (Mg Kα radiation with = 1253 eV) spectrometers). The composition of the products was also studied by diffuse reﬂectance IR spectroscopy for samples prepared on silicon and gallium arsenide powders of the same batches. An electron diffraction study of the structure of layers ~100 nm thick showed that all these layers were strongly amorphous. The conductivity of films was studied on high-alloy GaAs. A vacuum-deposited tin layer was used as a counter-electrode, because the work function of tin was close to that of GaAs."
