 "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was also utilised to examine both the as-received and water-plasma-treated polycarbonate surfaces before film deposition. Film thickness and refractive index were determined by spectroscopic ellipsometry (Model GESP-5, Sopra SA, France). The surface roughness of the as-received and water-plasma-treated samples were measured using a stylus profilometer (Alpha Step 200, Tencor). The contact angle of pure distilled water drops on the surface of the as-received and water-plasma-treated polycarbonate substrates was examined using an in-house built device. Nano-indentation with a 1 μm diamond spheroconical indenter was used to determine the biaxial Young's modulus of the films. Microtensile tests on coated samples were conducted with a small mechanical tester equipped with a 2500 N capacity load cell. The in situ observations indicated different interfacial behaviour between water-plasma-treated and non-treated samples. The tensile tests were conducted in a micromechanical tester positioned under an optical microscope allowing in situ viewing of cracking damage. The strain to initiate first cracking and the crack density as a function of strain were obtained. The in situ observations indicated different interfacial behaviour between water-plasma-treated and non-treated samples. The interfacial adhesion was examined using SEM (JEOL 6300)."
