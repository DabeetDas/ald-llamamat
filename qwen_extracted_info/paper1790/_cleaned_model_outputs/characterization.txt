  "evidence": "X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS) confirmed the presence of W2N phase in the film. X-ray Diffraction (XRD) and High Resolution Transmission Electron Microscopy (HR-TEM) depicted the as-deposited film to be amorphous which got converted to h-W2N upon annealing. X-ray Reflectivity (XRR) and Ellipsometry measurements were used to determine the film thickness, roughness and electron density. Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM) was used to study the surface morphology and RMS roughness. Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) was used to investigate the surface bound species after each individual ALD half-cycle. Quartz Crystal Microbalance (QCM) was used to study the growth rate. Hall Measurements were used to determine the bulk carrier density and resistivity. Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) was used to study the surface morphology of the as-deposited WNx film on bare SS substrate and the film on drop-cast CNT on SS substrate."
