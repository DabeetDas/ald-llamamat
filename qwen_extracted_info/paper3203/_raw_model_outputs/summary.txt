  "evidence": "The use of PMAM as an ALD resist offers good selectivity and high spatial resolution and provides a new, convenient route toward achieving spatially patterned film growth. The PMAM layer was simply removed just by dipping in water. After the removal process, the PMAM-coated surface exhibited the same properties as the original SiO2 surface.
