 "evidence": "The deposition behaviors at various temperatures were observed via a field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM, JEM-7001F JEOL). The atomic composition of the films, as controlled by varying the deposition temperature, was evaluated by an energy dispersive X-ray spectroscope (EDS) equipped on the FESEM and on a high-resolution transmission electron microscope (HRTEM-EDS, JEM-2100 JEOL). The surface roughness was analyzed by atomic force microscopy (AFM, Nanosurf AG, Switzerland). The crystallinity of the films obtained at each deposition temperature was analyzed by X-ray diffraction (XRD) measurements (Ultima IV, RIGAKU). Ex-situ sheet resistance (RS) measurements of the films deposited on the SiO2 substrate were conducted using a four-point probe (CMT-SR 200 N, Korea). In addition, simple test devices were fabricated to measure the current – voltage (I – V) characteristics and switching behaviors by using a semiconductor parameter analyzer (B1500A, Agilent)."
