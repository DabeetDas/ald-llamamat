  "evidence": "The microstructures of epitaxial SnO2 (rutile) thin films deposited by atomic layer deposition on α-Al2O3 (0 1 2) substrates at 600/C14C using either SnCl4 or SnI4 as tin precursor have been investigated by X-ray diffraction and transmission electron microscopy.
