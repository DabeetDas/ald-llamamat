  "evidence": "The properties of the PEALD-RuSiN films were analyzed using various tools: sheet resistance with a four-point probe, thickness by cross-sectional view transmission electron microscopy (XTEM), composition and impurities by Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS), chemical bonding by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), and microstructure using plan-view TEM and XTEM. Finally, Cu was electrodeposited directly onto an 8 nm thick PEALD-RuSiN film to test the feasibility of the film as a diffusion barrier for seedless Cu interconnects. The plan-view TEM bright-field image of the PEALD-Ru film showed crystalline grains approximately 10–20 nm in size. The plan-view TEM image of the RuSiN film deposited with the SiNx unit cycles of 5 showed relatively dark-contrast grains, 3 nm in size, which appeared to be uniformly distributed with bright-contrast regions separating the dark-contrast grains.
