  "evidence": "In the process temperature range of 310 −430 °C, a self-limiting surface reaction behavior with 4 × 103 L of 3CDS exposure and 2 × 106 L of NH3 plasma exposure was observed. 3CDS particularly gives ∼45 and ∼20% higher growth per cycle than HCDS and PCDS, respectively. In addition, the SiN x films deposited using 3CDS at 480 °C have improved the wet etch rate (0.4 nm/min in 200:1 HF) and density (2.88 g/cm3)."
