The self-limiting nature of the surface reactions allows precise control of the thickness of the deposited layers and gives rise to films with highly conformal step coverage. High quality dielectrics have been deposited at temperatures as low as 100°C. Resistivities of 10^17 Ω·cm, breakdown strengths of 8 × 10^6 V/cm, and interface-state densities of 10^11 states/eV·cm² have already been achieved and they suggest possible applications as a gate insulator or a dielectric passivation layer."
