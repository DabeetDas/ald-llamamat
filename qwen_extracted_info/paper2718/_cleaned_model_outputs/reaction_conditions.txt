  "evidence": "The plasma pulse plays an important role in the PE-ALD process of Co on silicon surfaces. The early cycles were characterized by the onset of Co—O bonds. The formation of Co—O bonds can be excluded at all substrate temperatures because no O 1s peak was detected between 529.9 and 531.1 eV. The H2/N2 plasma pulse provides active binding sites for the adsorption of precursor molecules due to the formation of Si-Nx or Si-NxOy surfaces. The N-containing species might adsorb during plasma exposure and are used during the subsequent precursor pulse to remove part of the precursor ligands. The NHx species increases when the H2 fraction in the H2/N2 plasma decreased. The Cp—Co bond was being easily broken under an H2/N2 plasma with a reducing atmosphere. This suggests that a Cp ligand reacts with the H2/N2 species and stable and volatile byproducts, such as are NHx and CHx, are released."
