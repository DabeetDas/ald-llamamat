Zr addition leads to changes in film microstructure and grain-size distribution. Hf xZr1−xO2 films have smaller and more uniform grain size compared to HfO2 for all deposition temperatures explored here. A monotonic decrease in band gap is observed as ZrO2 content is increased. The chlorin impurity in the films is strongly dependent on deposition temperature and independent of film composition. TEM images of transistors showed excellent thermal stability as revealed by a sharp Hf xZr1−xO2/Si interface and no Zr silicide formation. Significant improvement in device properties such as lower electrical thickness, higher permittivities, lower threshold voltage shift after stress, improved reliability, and higher mobilities are observed with Zr addition into HfO2."
