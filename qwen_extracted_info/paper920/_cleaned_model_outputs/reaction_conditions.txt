  "evidence": "A major issue with the ALD of silicates using SiCl4 as a precursor is the fact that the energy barrier for SiCl4 adsorption on Si-OH terminated surfaces is rather high, on the order of 20 kcal/mol. This leads to very slow SiCl4 chemisorption kinetics on Si-OH surfaces at common ALD temperatures. By contrast, calculations based on density-functional theory indicated a more than twofold lower energy barrier for SiCl4 on Ta-OH surfaces, similar to the case of HfSiOx ALD using HfCl4 and SiCl4 with H2O as oxidizer. Efficient chemisorption of SiCl4 can thus be expected to be possible on Ta-OH covered surfaces. Indeed, our experiments demonstrate that the enhanced chemisorption of SiCl4 occurs in combination with TaCl5/H2O ALD. While no deposition was observed for SiCl4/H2O cycles only, TaSiOx formation was observed as a result of SiOx incorporation when n:m TaCl5/H2O–SiCl4/H2O supercycles were used."
