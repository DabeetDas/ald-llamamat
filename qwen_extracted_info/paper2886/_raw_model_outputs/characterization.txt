  "evidence": "The process conditions of ALD were established based on UHV-TPD results, and HfO2 thin films were deposited on Si under these conditions followed by the analysis of the electric properties of the prepared film. An interlayer formed between the HfO2 film and the Si substrate was identified by in-situ X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) and by the analysis of residue adsorbed onto the substrate during the process. The thickness of the obtained thin film was measured using an ellipsometer (Gaetner Auto Gain Ellipsometer L116B) and the composition was determined using a scanning Auger microprobe system (Perkin-Elmer 660) and X-ray diffraction (XRD; Cu Kα, MAC Science M18XHF-SRA). A capacitor consisting of 6.25 × 10−2 mm2-Pt electrode and the prepared HfO2 onto Si was manufactured by e-beam evaporation for the measurement of the capacitance–voltage (C–V) and leakage current characteristics. The C–V curves were obtained using a system consisting of a probe station (Micromanipulator MM-410C12) and a plotter (Hewlett-Packard HP4280A), and the leakage current characteristics were determined using a system that consisted of a probe station (Cascade Microtech REL-3200) and a current analyzer (Hewlett-Packard HP4155A). The depth profile of a 99-Å-thick film obtained after 100 cycles of the ALD at 225 °C was analyzed."
