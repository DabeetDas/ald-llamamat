  "evidence": "Crystallographic characterisation of the germanium-doped ZnO films was performed using X-ray diffraction (XRD) and room temperature photoluminescence spectroscopy. Electron microscopy was performed using a JEOL 2100F in bright field illumination mode. Energy dispersive X-ray analysis of the samples were made using a JEOL 2000FX equipped with EDAX Genesis 4000 X-Ray system to measure the composition. Film thicknesses were estimated using a Rudolph Auto EL IV ellipsometer. Hall effect measurements to obtain carrier concentration and mobility were made on home-made equipment. Electrical resistivity measurements were made with a four-point probe system. The optical properties of the ZnO:Ge films were assessed by UV-Vis spectrophotometry. The PL signal from the Ge films is governed by direct band gap transitions."
