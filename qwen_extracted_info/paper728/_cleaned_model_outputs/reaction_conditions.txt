  "evidence": "The underlying growth reaction mechanism of the H2O-based ZTO ALD was ligand exchange upon H-transfer reactions. The GPC of ZnO was found to be reduced on SnOx surfaces; by contrast, the GPC of SnOx was enhanced on ZnO surfaces, which is consistent with our observations. This phenomenon could be explained by the change in the areal density of reaction sites after each metal precursor half-cycle. In other words, OH-terminated sites are diminished in number after the SnOx cycle and are replenished after the ZnO cycle. O2 plasma ALD has been documented to involve the mixed chemistry of H2O- and O3-based ALD, which dominantly produces the surface reactive sites such as hydroxyl groups and metal carbonates, respectively, after O2 plasma half-cycle. Rai et al. reported that metal carbonate species on the surface is relatively short-term intermediate which easily converted into metal oxide by producing CO2 gas during O2 PEALD, and hence the contribution of metal carbonate on the GPC as well as precursor chemisorption are quite small compared to hydroxyl group."
