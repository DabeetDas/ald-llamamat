  "evidence": "Amorphous ZnSnOx (ZTO) films were prepared using plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition (PEALD) in a temperature range of 100–200 ◦C. Metal-organic precursors of Sn(dmamp)2 and diethylzinc were employed as sources of Sn and Zn, respectively, in combination with O2 plasma as a reactant. Sn levels in the ZTO films were controlled by varying the SnO2/ZnO cycle ratio from 0 to 8. Grazing-angle X-ray diffraction revealed that the PEALD ZTO films possess an amorphous structure, irrespective of Sn levels from 20 to 59 at.%. ZTO films with intermediate Sn at.% exhibited smooth surface morphology compared to binary ZnO and SnO2 films. Additionally, the step coverage of a ZTO film deposited on hole pattern with an aspect ratio of 8 and opening diameter of nm was about 93%, suggesting the realisation of self-limited growth."
