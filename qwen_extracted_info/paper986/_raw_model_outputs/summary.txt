  "evidence": "Zinc oxysulﬁde has been grown by spatial atomic layer deposition (S-ALD) and successfully applied as buffer layer in Cu(In, Ga)Se2 (CIGS) solar cells. Diethylzinc, water and hydrogen sulﬁte (H2S) have been used as zinc, oxygen and sulfur precursor, respectively. The S/(S + O) ratio in the ﬁlm is accurately controlled by exposing the substrate simultaneously to both H2O and H2S precursors, which are pre-mixed and co-injected in the same deposition zone. An optimum value of S/(S + O) ratio of about 0.4 is found for which both the short circuit current density (Jsc = 34.2 mA/cm2) and cell efficiency (η = 15.9%) increase, as compared to reference cells with CdS buffer layer (Jsc = 32.1 mA/cm2, η = 15.5%)."
