  "evidence": "The aim of the present work is to study the thermal oxidation of silicon carbide specimens whose surface layer contains oxide additions of chromium, vanadium, phosphorus, titanium, and silicon, obtained by the molecular layering (ML) method. In order to apply the oxide layers to the surface of SiC by the molecular layering method we used chlorine-containing low-molecular-weight compounds of these elements: SiCl4, POCl3, VOCl3, TiCl4, and CrO2Cl2. The increase in the number of process cycles of SiC with VOCI3 vapors and H2O leads to an increase in the size of the first peak on the oxidation-rate curve... During the processing of SiC powder with chromium-containing compounds we note an increase in the resistance to thermal oxidation."
