  "evidence": "Microstructural and electrical properties of ALD ZrO2 on Ge were investigated using transmission electron microscopy (TEM), x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), and basic MOS electrical characterizations. The thickness and film microstructures of selected samples were analyzed by both cross-sectional and plan-view TEM. The compositional characterization of a ZrO2/Ge structure was carried out by angle resolved-XPS. The epitaxial relationship between film and substrate was verified using plan-view imaging and electron diffraction (ED) analysis. High-frequency C–V measurements were performed after the forming gas annealing. I–V measurements were performed using a Keithley 230 programmable voltage source and 6512 programmable electrometer."
