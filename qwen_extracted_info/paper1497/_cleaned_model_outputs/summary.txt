  "evidence": "In this study a new way of depositing thin films of LiF using atomic layer deposition (ALD) is presented. Mg(thd)2, TiF4 and Lithd were used as precursors, and they produced crystalline LiF at a temperature range of 300 −350 °C. The films were studied by UV −vis spectrometry, field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM), X-ray diffraction (XRD), atomic force microscopy (AFM), time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (ToF-ERDA), and energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX). According to the ToF-ERDA measurements, the films are pure LiF with very small Mg and Ti impurities, the largest impurity being hydrogen with contents below 1 atom %."
