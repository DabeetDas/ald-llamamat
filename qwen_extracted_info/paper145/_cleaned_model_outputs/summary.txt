  "evidence": "Titanium dioxide films are grown with radical-enhanced atomic layer deposition (RE-ALD) from titanium isopropoxide and oxygen radicals at between 50 and 300 °C on silicon, glass, platinum, and RuO2 surfaces. The oxygen radicals are produced by dissociating molecular oxygen with a remote microwave plasma discharge. The films grown at 50 °C have a dielectric constant of 20, and undergo catastrophic breakdown at 0.6 MV cm–1 electric field. The film density and refractive index are 3.2 g cm–3 and 2.2, respectively, for a film deposited at 50 °C, and increases to 3.8 g cm–3 and 2.4, respectively, for a film deposited at 250 °C. All the films grown below 250 °C are amorphous according to X-ray diffraction (XRD)."
