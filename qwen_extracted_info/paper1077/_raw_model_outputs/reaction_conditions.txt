  "evidence": "Based on these reports, the RuOx formation during Ru ALD can be partially explained. When the oxygen flow is large during the oxygen exposure step, the equilibrium oxygen pressure in the deposition chamber becomes high enough to form large number of subsurface oxygen atoms. Under this condition, all of the subsurface oxygen atoms are not consumed for the oxidation of Ru precursor ligands during the following Ru precursor exposure step. These excessive oxygen atoms form immobile Ru–O bonds, resulting in the formation of oxygen containing Ru phase. It should be noted that the number of subsurface oxygen atoms is likely to be determined by the oxygen partial pressure, not by the oxygen exposure. In other words, metallic Ru film is formed at low oxygen pressure even if the exposure time is long, since the number of generated subsurface oxygen atoms is not large. At the high growth temperature, however, the number of subsurface oxygen atoms becomes large at the same oxygen pressure and the formation of oxygen containing Ru phase becomes feasible."
