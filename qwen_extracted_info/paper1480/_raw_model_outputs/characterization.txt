Surface morphology of the films was examined by a Hitachi S-4800 field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM). Impurity contents of the films were determined with time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) by a 1.7 MV Pelletron accelerator using a 10.215 MeV 35Cl5+ ion beam and full Monte Carlo simulations. For atomic force microscopy (AFM) studies a Multimode V equipped with Nanoscope V controller (Veeco Instruments) was used. Thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) of OsCp 2 was done in a flowing nitrogen atmosphere (1 atm) with a heating rate of 10 °C/min by a Mettler Toledo Star system equipped with a TGA 850 thermobalance."
