  "evidence": "Atomic layer deposition (ALD) was used to deposit thin films of molybdenum nitride from a new precursor, bis(tert-butylimido)-bis(dimethylamido)molybdenum, and ammonia. Molybdenum nitride was successfully deposited on silicon, electrochemically grown nickel, chromium, and quartz glass. Growth per cycle was maintained over substrate materials of different types.
