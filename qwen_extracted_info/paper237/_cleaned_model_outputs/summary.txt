  "evidence": "The ZnO active layers for the oxide TFT devices were grown on SiO2 (200 nm)/p-Si substrates by ALD using Diethylzinc (DEZn) and H2O as the reagents. The thickness of the active layer was controlled by changing the number of cycles at a working temperature of 150 ◦C. The electrical properties of the as-grown and thermally annealed films were characterized by Hall effect measurements. The TFT with the 17 nm thick as-grown channel layer showed a saturation mobility of 1.7 cm2V−1s−1 and an on/off ratio of 3 × 106, while the annealed TFT had a saturation mobility of 1.3 cm2V−1s−1 and an on/off ratio of 9 × 105. The ALD-grown ZnO layers showed reduced electrical resistivity and carrier density after thermal treatment in oxygen."
