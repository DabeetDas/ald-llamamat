  "evidence": "A conformal titanium dioxide (TiO 2) layer was deposited onto chromium nitride (CrN) coated stainless steel by atomic layer deposition technique, and the electrochemical corrosion test on the CrN single-layer and TiO 2/CrN double-layer coated sample was carried out. The equilibrium corrosion potential of the double-layer coated sample shifted positively compare to that of the single-layer coated one.
