  "evidence": "X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) reveals that the TiO2 layer blocked the through-thickness cracks in the CrN layer. The surface morphology of the TiO2 and CrN films was examined using a Veeco Digital Instrument CPII atomic force microscope (AFM). A Siemens D500 X-ray diffractometer (XRD) with the 1.54 Å Cu Kα line as the excitation source was employed to examine the crystal structure of the films. A JY Raman spectrometer was used to measure the vibration spectrum of the samples. The corrosion behaviour of our samples was measured using a Solartron SI 1287 Electrochemical Interface system in a three-electrode cell with 3 wt.% sodium chloride (NaCl) aqueous solution as the electrolyte. The thickness of the films was measured using a Jobin-Yvon DeltaPsi ellipsometer. Potentiodynamic polarization measurements indicate that the corrosion behaviour of the CrN coated stainless steel is improved by introducing the additional TiO2 layer."
