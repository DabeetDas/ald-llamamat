  "evidence": "To prove the crystalline phases of the deposited films, x-ray diffraction (XRD) is applied using a PANalytical X’Pert PRO x-ray diffraction system. For the determination of the surface roughness, and to testify the homogeneity as well as the thickness of the deposited platinum films, atomic force microscopy (AFM) is applied using a Digital Instruments Dimensions 3100 AFM. High-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HR-TEM) studies at an FEI TECNAI F 20 system are performed to investigate the silicon /dielectric and dielectric/platinum interfaces. Chemical composition is measured by time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectroscopy (ToF-SIMS), performed at an Ion ToF-SIMS V instrument equipped with a 25 kV Ga+ liquid metal ion gun. The physical thickness of the deposited gate dielectrics is determined by using a spectroscopic ellipsometry setup GES-5 from Sopra. To extract equivalent oxide thickness and interface trap density from MOS capacitors [12] at room temperature, capacitance–voltage as well as admittance characteristics is measured using an agilent precision LCR-meter (4284A). This is done at a micro-needle prober station in a frequency range from 10 kHz to 1 MHz. Current–voltage characteristics are measured to investigate the gate leakage characteristics of the MOS gate stacks using an agilent semiconductor parameter analyzer (4156B)."
