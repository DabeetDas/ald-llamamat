The resulting LaPO4 ﬁlms are amorphous as deposited, and crystallize to the monoclinic structure after annealing in air for 10 h at 1350 °C. The LaPO4 thin ﬁlms can also be doped by calcium during growth by replacing some of the La(thd)3 pulses by Ca(thd)2."
