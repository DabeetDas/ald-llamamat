Transmission electron microscopy (TEM) was applied for selected samples to examine the interface layers between HfO2 and silicon. The composition of the interface layer was checked by energy dispersive x-ray (EDX) analysis. The residual contamination level and film composition were determined by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA). The electrical measurements were carried out on Al/HfO2/p-Si(100)/Al capacitor structures. Capacitance–voltage curves were recorded using a HP 4284A precision low capacitance resonator LCR meter. The existence of hysteresis is due to rechargeable traps in the oxide layer, with the charge carriers being mainly electrons which become ejected from the oxide at strongly negative bias voltages. X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS) was used to prove the significance of interfacial SiO2 in the films grown using higher O2 pressures."
