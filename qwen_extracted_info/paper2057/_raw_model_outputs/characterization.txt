The MEIS data were obtained using an incident beam of 100 keV H+, channeling in normal incidence. The backscattered ions were detected at a scattering angle of 120°. Nitrogen content in the interfacial layer was accurately measured by nuclear reaction analysis (NRA) using the 14N(sd,a1d12C nuclear reaction and MEIS. Physical characterization was complimented by electrical measurements. MOS capacitor structures with different areas ranging from 10−5 to 10−3 cm2 were fabricated with Al or W gate electrodes.
