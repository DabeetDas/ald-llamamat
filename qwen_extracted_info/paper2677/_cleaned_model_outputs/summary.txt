  "evidence": "The authors report the plasma enhanced atomic layer deposition (PEALD) of a nickel thin film using bis(1,4-diisopropyl-1,4-diazabutadiene)nickel [Ni(dpdab)2] and NH3 plasma. Ni(dpdab)2 showed the atomic layer deposition (ALD) process window between 125 and 150 /C14 C with the ALD growth of /C24 2A˚ /cycle. The resistivity of the ALD thin film decreased with increasing radio-frequency (RF) power, and lower resistivities with high RF powers are due to the lower carbon concentration and larger grain size. The minimum resistivity of the PEALD film at /C14 C in the ALD process window was 146 lX cm, which is significantly higher than bulk Ni resistivity (7.0 lX cm) mainly due to the nitrogen content ( /C24 13%) in the as-deposited film. For a lower nitrogen concentration, the PEALD film was annealed at 400 /C14 C under 1 Torr of H2 for 30 min, resulting in the reduction of resistivity from 146 to 13.3 lX cm and removal of nitrogen impurities."
