Sulfur L2,3 X-ray emission spectroscopy (XES) data were obtained at Beamline 8.0.1 of the Advanced Light Source (ALS), Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, using the newly installed iRIXS endstation (8.0.1.1). SEM was performed using a FEI Magellan 400 XHR at the Stanford Nano Shared Facility (SNSF). Optical transmission measurements were performed using a Varian Cary 6000i UV−vis−NIR spectrophotometer at the Stanford Soft Materials Facility (SMF). X-ray diffraction measurements were performed using a Philips PANalytical X’Pert Pro in parallel beam mode with Cu Kα radiation. Spectroscopic ellipsometry was used to determine the thicknesses of the TiO2 and MoOx layers. Linear sweep voltammetry (LSV) and chronoamperometry (CA) were used to assess the electrochemical performance. Incident photon-to-current conversion efficiency (IPCE) measurements were performed using a 1000 W Hg/Xe arc lamp and a Newport Oriel 74100 monochromator."
