 "evidence": "The Ga:O ratio was measured as 1:1.7 by the Rutherford backscattering spectrometry, and auger electron spectroscopy confirmed that there was no carbon impurity (under the detection limit). The surface morphology was investigated through an atomic force microscope analysis, and all of the films deposited at 150, 200, and 250 °C showed smooth and featureless characteristics. The crystal structures of the Ga2O3 films were examined by X-ray diffraction (XRD, Rigaku diffractometer) using two theta method with CuKα radiation (λ = 1.5418 Å). The surface morphology was observed using an atomic force microscope (AFM, Digital Instruments; Dimension 3100). The film transmittance was measured using ultraviolet visible spectroscopy (UV –VIS). Current–voltage (I–V) and capacitance–voltage (C–V) measurements were taken to investigate the breakdown voltage, leakage current level, and dielectric constant."
