  "evidence": "The hydrogende profiles were determined by Elastic Recoil Detection (ERD) using a 1.9 MeV He+ beam impinging on the sample under 15° glancing incidence, and 30° recoil angle. All other elements could be determined in a single run by Elastic Backscattering Spectrometry (EBS) with 2.8 MeV protons at perpendicular incidence. The crystal structure of the Li2CO3 layers was determined using a Philips X'Pert MPD diffractometer equipped with a Cu Kα source (1.54 Å radiation). The morphology of the deposited layers was investigated by the SEM which was described before. The film thickness and dielectric function were monitored in situ by spectroscopic ellipsometry (SE) with a J.A. Woollam, Inc. M2000U (0.75 –5.0 eV) tool. For electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) testing an Autolab (Metrohm) potentiostat with a frequency-response analyzer module (frequency range from 0.01 Hz to 1 MHz) was used."
