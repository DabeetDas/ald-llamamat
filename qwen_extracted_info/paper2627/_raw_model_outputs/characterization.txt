Signal integration as well as Quark # and SIMNRA # fitting software were used for data analysis. STEM EDX line scans were measured using an FEI Titan 60–300 equipped with a DCOR probe spherical aberration corrector and a SuperX windowless silicon drift detector. Film structure was characterized by x-ray diffraction using a Bruker-AXS Microdiffractometer. Optical properties were measured on an Ocean Optics UV-Vis spectrometer. Hall measurements were made using a Quantum Design Model 6000 Physical Property Measurement System. Cross-sectional transmission electron microscopy (TEM) of a ZTO film validated the spectroscopic ellipsometry measurement used in determining film thickness. In addition to the TEM images showing no evidence of grains in the amorphous film, STEM HAADF EDX line scans were collected for elemental composition to compare to the RBS measurements."
