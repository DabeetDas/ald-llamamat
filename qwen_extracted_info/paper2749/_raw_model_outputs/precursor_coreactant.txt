  "evidence": "The properties of titanium nitride deposited by atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) using three different deposition processes, i.e., TiI4/NH3, TiCl4/NH3 and TiCl4/Zn/NH3, as a diffusion barrier between copper and silicon were investigated. Without the additional reducing agent Zn, the TiCl4–NH3 process resulted in a resistivity of about 250 mV cm which still compares favorably with TiN films deposited by CVD from the same precursors and at the same temperature. On the other hand, when TiI4 was used as a titanium precursor, a low resistivity of 70 mV cm was obtained at 500 °C without using any reducing agent other than NH3. Also, the excellent conformality of the ALE deposited TiN films has been verified. We have also shown that high purity copper can be deposited at 250 °C by ALE using copper (II)2,2,6,6-tetramethyl-3,5-heptanedionate, Cu(thd)2, as a precursor and hydrogen as a reducing agent."
