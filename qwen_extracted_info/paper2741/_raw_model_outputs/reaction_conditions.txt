  "evidence": "The authors suggest the following model. In the first stage of TMG introduction period on arsenic terminated GaAs surface, gallium atoms chemically bond with the arsenic atoms, a two-dimensional gallium monolayer growing on the GaAs substrate. Once the surface is covered with gallium atoms, additional gallium atoms from vapor phase may form gallium clusters or gallium droplets. When arsine gas is introduced to this gallium surface, arsenic atoms can quickly bond with the two-dimensional gallium atoms and form a monolayer of gallium arsenide. The fast gallium arsenide monolayer growth corresponds to this stage. Arsenic atoms also react with the gallium atoms from the gallium clusters or gallium droplet but a much longer time is needed for the formation of a GaAs layer. This corresponds to the slow growth rate once 1 ML has been formed."
