  "evidence": "In this Communication we show that with spatially separated ALD of Al2O3, growth rates of 1.2 nm s−1 can be achieved, showing excellent surface passivation (surface recombination velocities of < 2 cm s−1). The main advantage of spatially separating the half-reactions is that the purge steps between the precursor dosages in conventional ALD become obsolete. The optimum deposition temperature as well as post-deposition anneal temperature for thermal ALD of Al2O3 passivation layers has been determined to be 200 °C and 350 °C, respectively."
