The films are deposited on Si(100) using Er and Hf precursors, with an Er content of 15% achieved by tuning the ALD pulse ratio. The films are annealed at 900 °C, leading to improved dielectric properties, including a higher dielectric constant and reduced leakage currents compared to pure HfO2.   "evidence": "Films were grown in a Savannah 200 ALD reactor at 300 °C on H-terminated n-type Si(100) using iPrCp3Er and MeCp2HfMe(OMe) as Er and Hf sources, respectively, and O3 as the oxygen source. A high temperature postdeposition rapid thermal annealing (RTA) was performed at selected temperatures between 400 and 900 °C. The dielectric constant increased to 33 after RTA at 900 °C due to the increase in the crystallized fraction, the reduction of microstrain and of the OH− content."
