  "evidence": "In the flow-type reactors, ALD processes are usually characterized and optimized indirectly by varying the process parameters (temperature, exposure and purge times, reactant pressures, etc.) and observing afterward their effects on the film properties. Mass spectrometry is a very promising way to achieve this and has been utilized, for example, in chemical vapor deposition (CVD) studies. However, mass spectrometry studies on ALD processes in flow-type reactors require rather complicated differentially pumped sampling setups, which is evidently the reason for only sparse utilization of mass spectrometry in the field of ALD until now. In this work, we have used the recently developed quadrupole mass spectrometer-ALD reactor setup for characterizing the TMA-water ALD process. Several ALD oxide processes have been studied by quartz crystal microbalance (QCM), which gives real time results from the weight changes on the crystal surface and helps in the interpretation of the growth mechanisms."
