  "evidence": "The surface reactions between trimethylaluminum (TMA) and deuterated water (D2O) in the deposition of Al2O3 were studied by using a mass spectrometer to determine the amount of the reaction product, methane (CH3D), produced in a flow-type atomic layer deposition (ALD) reactor during each deposition step.
