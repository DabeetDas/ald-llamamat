A hole-pattern structure image was obtained by field-emission transmission electron microscopy (FE-TEM, FEI, TALOS F200X), operating at 200 kV. Kelvin probe force microscopy (KPFM, Asylum Research, MFM-3D) was used to investigate the work function and topography of the thin films. A Ti/Ir-coated silicon tip was used with a spring constant of 2.8 Nm-1, a resonant frequency of 75 kHz and a tip radius of 25 nm. Highly pure Pt thin films were also confirmed by auger electron spectroscopy (AES) analysis, corroborated to the XPS results."
