  "evidence": "The structure of ruthenium films was determined by grazing incidence X-ray diffraction (GIXRD) studies mainly carried out by means of a PANalytical X’Pert PRO diffractometer with 1 1 incidence angle, or by high-energy electron diffraction (RHEED). The film thicknesses were measured either by X-ray reflection (XRR) method or by an energy dispersive X-ray (EDX) spectrometer connected to scanning electron microscope (SEM) Hitachi S-4800. The sheet resistance of the Ru films was evaluated by four-point probe Cascade Microtech C4S-44. The surface roughness was studied by an Autoprobe CP atomic force microscope (AFM), Park Scientific Instruments. Transmission electron microscopy (TEM) using a field emission gun TECNAI F30 ST operated at 300 kV was applied to examine the quality of selected Ru films, as well as formation of possible interfacial layers. X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was performed on selected films using synchrotron radiation at the U49/2-PGM2 beamline at BESSY-II."
