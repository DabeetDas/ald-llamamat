  "evidence": "XPS measures the core electron binding energies of atomic species that exist within the near-surface region (~5.0 nm). The core binding energy is dependent on the chemical environment and can be used to characterize thin films. Low-resolution, broad energy scans, and high-resolution measurements of the Al(2p), Al(2s), N(1s), O(1s), and C(1s) regions were taken on each sample.
