  "evidence": "The atomic layer deposition growth of tungsten nitride films was demonstrated using the precursors W2(NMe2)6 and ammonia with substrate temperatures between 150 and 250 uC. At 180 uC, surface saturative growth was achieved with W2(NMe2)6 pulse lengths of 2.0 s. The growth rates were between 0.74 and 0.81 A˚ cycle21 at substrate temperatures between 180 and 210 uC. Films grown at 180 and 210 uC exhibited resistivity values between 810 and 4600 mV cm. The as-deposited films were amorphous. Amorphous films containing a protective AlN overlayer were annealed to 600–800 uC under a nitrogen atmosphere. X-Ray diffraction patterns suggested that crystallization does not occur at or below 800 uC."
