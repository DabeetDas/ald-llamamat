  "evidence": "By employing plasma-assisted atomic layer deposition, thin films of Al2O3 and TiN are subsequently deposited in a single reactor at a single substrate temperature with the objective of fabricating high-quality TiN/Al2O3/p-Si metal-oxide-semiconductor capacitors. Transmission electron microscopy and Rutherford backscattering spectroscopy analyses show well-defined interfaces and good Al2O3 stoichiometry, respectively. Electrical investigation of as-deposited test structures demonstrates leakage current densities as low as 1 nA/cm2. Current-voltage measurements demonstrate clear Fowler–Nordheim tunneling with an average TiN/Al2O3 barrier height of 3.3 eV. Steep Weibull distributions of the breakdown electric field around 7.5 MV/cm indicate good reliability of these devices."
