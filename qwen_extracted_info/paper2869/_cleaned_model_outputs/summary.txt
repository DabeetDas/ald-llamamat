  "evidence": "The growth of ZnO:Al films was carried out in a traveling wave type hot wall reactor at the substrate temperature of 180 /C14C. For the growth of ZnO film, diethylzinc (DEZ) and H2O were used as the zinc and oxygen precursors, respectively. These precursors were alternatively injected into the reactor using nitrogen as a carrier gas. The surface work function of the ZnO:Al films and ITO film was measured using a photoelectron spectrometer surface analyzer (RIKEN AC-2). The devices fabricated with them exhibited higher current and luminance with higher external quantum efficiencies below approximately 5000 cd/m2, than those fabricated with a conventional tin-doped indium oxide (ITO) anode."
