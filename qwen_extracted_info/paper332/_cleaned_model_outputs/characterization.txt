 "evidence": "The X-ray diffraction patterns of the films were recorded with a Philips powder diffractometer also using Cu Ka radiation. The MS films were also characterized by Auger spectrometry. The S Ka intensities of a ZnS sample with known thickness and that of MS (M = Ca, Sr, Ba) were compared and the thickness of the MS films was calculated by taking the densities and molecular weights into account. There is a distinct oxygen peak in the Auger spectra. The alkaline earth sulfides are quite sensitive to atmospheric moisture. The samples were not specially protected between the film growth and the Auger measurements but they were exposed to air with medium humidity."
