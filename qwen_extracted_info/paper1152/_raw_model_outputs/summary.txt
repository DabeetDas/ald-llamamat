  "evidence": "Atomic layer deposition (ALD) of ZrO2 thin ﬁlms was investigated using a linked cyclopentadienyl-amido compound of zirconium, { η5:η1-Cp(CH2)3NMe}Zr(NMe2)2 with ozone. The ALD-window was established as 300 – 380 °C with a growth rate of about 0.95 Å/cycle. A good ﬁlm conformality of 0.95 for 5.7 –6.1 nm-thick ﬁlm was obtained for bottom and sidewall step coverages of 0.93 and 0.95, respectively. The ALD at 330 °C yielded a ZrO2 having good crystallinity. The ﬁlm showed low impurity levels and a strong tendency to form the tetragonal/cubic phases despite a low ﬁlm thickness of 8.58 nm (root-mean-square roughness = 0.49 nm). Better ALD performance was obtained with this linked precursor than with the commonly used CpZr(NMe 2)3 precursor."
