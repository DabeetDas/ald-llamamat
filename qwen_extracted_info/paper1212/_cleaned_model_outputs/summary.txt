  "evidence": "A plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition (PEALD) process using the Ag(fod)(PEt3) precursor in combination with NH3 plasma was reported. The steady growth rate of the reported process ((0.24 ± 0.03) nm/cycle) was found to be six times larger than that of the previously reported Ag ALD process based on the same precursor in combination with H2 plasma ((0.04 ± 0.02) nm/cycle). The deposited Ag films were polycrystalline fcc Ag for both processes. The film morphology was investigated by ex situ scanning electron microscopy and grazing-incidence small-angle X-ray scattering, and it was found that films grown with the NH3 plasma process exhibit a much higher particle areal density and smaller particle sizes on oxide substrates compared to those deposited using the H2 plasma process."
