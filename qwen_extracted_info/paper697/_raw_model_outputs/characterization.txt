 "evidence": "The morphologies of the ZnO nanostructures were characterized by SEM (Leo 1530). The ZnO–Al 2O3 core–shell nanostructures and Al 2O3 shelled nanostructures were transferred to carbon coated copper grid and characterized by TEM (Hitachi 2000 and JEOL 100C). The chemical composition of the ZnO–Al2O3 core–shell nanostructure and Al 2O3 nanotubes were analyzed by the energy dispersive X-ray (EDX) spectroscopy equipped on the Hitachi TEM. The TEM image indicates that the alumina shell thickness is about 11 nm with 100 growth cycles. The SAED pattern indicates that ZnO nanowire is grown along (0 0 0 1) direction. The microstructure of the tubular products was characterized by TEM. The contrast of the TEM images indicates that the Al2O3 nanotubes are hollow. The energy dispersive X-ray spectrum from the Al 2O3 tubular nanowire shown in Fig. 4 (D) demonstrates the presence of O, Al in the structure, Cu and C from the copper grid."
