  "evidence": "Ruthenium-titanium nitride (Ru–TiN) thin films were grown by plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition (PEALD) at a growth temperature of 200°C. For the Ru–TiN PEALD, Ru and TiN were sequentially deposited to intermix TiN with Ru. The microstructures of Ru–TiN films changed from polycrystalline to amorphous, as the intermixing ratio of Ru increased in the deposited Ru–TiN films. Amorphous Ru–TiN barriers showed a superior Cu diffusion barrier property to TiN or Ru film itself. Moreover, Ru–TiN films showed a good adhesion to both chemical vapor deposition copper and an underlayer of SiO2."
