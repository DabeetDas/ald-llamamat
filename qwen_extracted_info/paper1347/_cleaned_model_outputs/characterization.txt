  "evidence": "Crystalline nickel metal was observed by X -ray diffraction for a 60 nm thick film deposited at 180 °C. Films with thicknesses of 18 and 60 nm grown at 180 °C showed low rms roughnesses (<2.5% of thicknesses) by atomic force microscopy. X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy of 18 and 60 nm thick films deposited on platinum at 180 °C revealed ionizations consistent with nickel metal after sputtering with argon ions. Cross-sectional SEM revealed continuous films on ruthenium substrates. The nickel 2p ionization region of the 18 nm thick film is shown. XPS depth profiling results are shown. Cross-sectional SEM analysis showed film growth on the platinum feature. The film thickness was determined by SEM. EDS was used to analyze the platinum stripe region and the Si(100) region."
