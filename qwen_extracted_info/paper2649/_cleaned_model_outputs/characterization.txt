  "evidence": "The thickness of the Ru films was determined by x-ray reflectance (XRR, PANalytical X’-pert MRD using Cu-Ka radiation at 1.5 kW). The film resistivity was determined from the sheet resistance of the Ru films measured by a four-point probe, and their thicknesses were determined by XRR. Grazing-incidence angle (incident angle,h ¼ 3/C14 ) x-ray diffraction (GIAXRD, PANalytical X’-pert MRD) analysis was performed to identify the phase and crystallinity. The impurities in the films, such as oxygen and carbon, were detected by secondary ion mass spectroscopy (SIMS, CAMECA IMS-6f in Korea Basic Science Institute) depth profiling. The step coverage of the process was evaluated by coating ultrathin films onto nanoscale trenches (AR: /C24 4.5 and top opening width of 25 nm) or holes (AR: /C24 40 and top opening diameter of 40 nm) and by characterizing those films by cross-sectional view TEM (XTEM)."
