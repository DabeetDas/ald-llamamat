  "evidence": "The oxidation mechanism of FeCp2 by ozone is quite complicated. It could be possible that the mixed phase composition is a result of the ALD process carried out at temperatures relatively low for the deposition of iron oxide, thus slowing down the surface reactions. The ALD of a-Fe2O3 from FeCp2 and O3 can generally be regarded as a rather high-temperature process, and the substrate temperatures 300–350 1C (otherwise most common for the ALD of different oxides) are in this case already close to the reaction threshold. It is possible, that the formation of a-Fe2O3 in the reaction between FeCp-terminated surface and O3 proceeds via initial formation of Fe(II) oxide, FeO, followed by the formation of Fe3O4 until complete oxidation to a-Fe2O3. In this case, the magnetite phase can occur just as a residual phase with stoichiometry characteristic of incompletely oxidized iron oxide."
