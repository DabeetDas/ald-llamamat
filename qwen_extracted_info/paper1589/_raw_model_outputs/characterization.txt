  "evidence": "The nature of the species deposited by each half-reaction was explored first. Evidence was obtained for the partial loss of chlorine atoms and the reduction of the metal during the adsorption of the TiCl4. The data suggest that the reduction of the Ti4+ species may therefore occur during the TiCl4, not NH3, dosing step. The incorporation of impurities in the films was also investigated. Chlorine is only deposited on the surface, and in negligible quantities. This Cl appears to originate from readsorption of the HCl byproduct, and could be removed by light sputtering, heating, or further ammonia treatment. Oxygen incorporation, on the other hand, was unavoidable and was determined to possibly come from diffusion from the underlying substrate."
