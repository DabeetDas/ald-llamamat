  "evidence": "In this study, atomic layer deposition (ALD) of Cu2S was explored using bis(dimethylamino-2-methyl-2-butoxy)copper(II) and 5% H2S combination as Cu and S sources, respectively. It was revealed that stoichiometric Cu2S films could be deposited at 120-150 oC, while sulfur deficient films was formed at 200 oC. Cu2S ALD process at low temperatures of 100-120 oC resulted in continuous film formation while the higher deposition temperatures of >150 oC led to island formation."
