Elemental compositions of the ﬁlms were determined with time-of-ﬂight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) by a 5 MV tandem accelerator using a 48 MeV Br9+ ion beam. Resistivities of the iridium thin ﬁlms were calculated from sheet resistances measured with a four-point probe technique and from the ﬁlm thicknesses."
