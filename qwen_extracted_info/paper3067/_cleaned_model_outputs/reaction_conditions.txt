  "evidence": "The ALD reaction of lead oxide growth might proceed via the chemisorption of precursors at the hydroxy group on the surface. The higher chemisorption of H2O corresponds to the higher growth rate of the films during the initial growth stage. The reason for the higher level of H2O chemisorption during the initial stage of the growth might be related to the catalytic activity of Ir. Once the Ir surface is covered by the films up to 1 nm thick, the catalytic activity of the Ir surface disappears and the growth rate decreases. The lower oxidation power of H2O might prevent the complete oxidation of incorporated Pb atoms at such a low growth temperature."
