  "evidence": "Chemical compositions of the as-deposited thin films were calculated from X-ray fluorescence spectroscopy (XRF; Philips model PW 1480 spectrometer; Rh tube) measurement data with de Jongh Kappa model (Uniquant 4.34 program, Omega Data Systems, Netherlands). The chemical composition was also confirmed for selected samples with inductively coupled-plasma optical-emission spectrometry (ICP-OES; Perkin-Elmer ICP-OES, 7100 DV) measurements. Crystallinity of the post-deposition annealed films was examined by grazing incidence X-ray diffraction (GIXRD; PANanalytical model X’pert Pro diffractometer, Cu K/H9251 radiation) and the film thickness with X-ray reflectivity (XRR; PANanalytical model X’pert Pro diffractometer, Cu K/H9251 radiation). The film thickness was determined with both the direct calculation method and the Fourier method, using the average of observed values from multiple measurement points. Morphology was investigated for the as-deposited film with atomic force microscopy (AFM; Veeco Dimension 5000 SPM) measurements carried out in the tapping mode. For the post-annealed films, room-temperature Seebeck coefficient was measured using a steady-state method and resistivity using a four-probe method from room temperature to 600 ◦C. Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) analysis was performed (Nicolet, Magna-IR Spectrometer 750) to the relevant samples deposited on silicon, to study the possible carbonyl impurities of the deposited and post-annealed films."
