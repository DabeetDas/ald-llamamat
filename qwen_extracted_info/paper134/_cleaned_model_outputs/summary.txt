  "evidence": "Thin films of Co3O4 have been successfully deposited on SiO2/Si(100) and MgO(001) substrates by atomic layer deposition (ALD) using the precursor combination CoI2/O2. The deposition temperature was found to have a strong influence on the growth rate. The films were found to grow as the cubic Co3O4 phase throughout the temperature range 475–700 °C, polycrystalline on SiO2/Si(100), and epitaxial on MgO(001). No iodine could be detected by Rutherford backscattering spectroscopy (RBS) or by X-ray fluorescence (XRF) spectroscopy in any of the deposited films."
