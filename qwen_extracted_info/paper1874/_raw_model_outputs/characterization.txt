Spectra of the films grown were measured within a wavelength range of 370–1100 nm using a Hitachi U-2000 spectrophotometer. Structural analysis was carried out with a Philips MPD 1880 powder X-ray diffractometer using Cu Kα radiation. The depth profiling of the film composition and residual contaminants was carried out using time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA). A 34 MeV I91 beam for TOF-ERDA was obtained from a 5 MV tandem accelerator EGP-10-II at the Accelerator Laboratory at the University of Helsinki. The chlorine contamination level was measured also by a Link ISIS Energy Dispersive X-ray spectrometer (EDX) installed to a Zeiss DSM 962 scanning electron microscope. For the electrical measurements, MOS capacitors were defined by electron beam evaporating circular platinum electrodes through a shadow mask.
