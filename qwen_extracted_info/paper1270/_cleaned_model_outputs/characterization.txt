Time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectroscopy (TOFYSIMS) and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) are explored to study the chemical composition of the deposited ALD films on Si(111) and glass substrates respectively. The total optical transmission is obtained using a Lambda 950 UVYNIR visible spectrometer. Electrical measurements are performed in four-point probe Van der Pauw geometry using Lakeshore 8404 AC/DC Hall Measurement System. The characteristic self-saturation behavior is one of the salient features of any ALD mechanism. The growth rate obtained by QCM experiments is further verified by exAsitu XRR measurement.
