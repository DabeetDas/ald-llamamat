  "evidence": "The crystallinity of the films was analyzed with a PANalytical X’Pert Pro MPD x-ray diffractometer using CuKα-radiation. High temperature x-ray diffraction (HTXRD) measurements were conducted in an Anton-Paar HTK1200N oven under nitrogen atmosphere between 25 and 975 °C. Film thicknesses and refractive indices were determined from reflectance spectra between 370 and 1100 nm measured by a Hitachi U-2000 spectrophotometer. The coatings were analyzed with a Hitachi S-4800 field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM) and Zeiss Sigma FESEM combined with energy dispersive x-ray spectroscopy (EDX) instrument. Elementary concentration profiles were determined by the time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (ToF-ERDA). The adhesion of the films to the substrate was examined by the Scotch tape test. The stability of the coatings in physiological environment and the biocompatibility and adhesion strength of the films were also evaluated."
