  "evidence": "The characteristics of the PEALD hafnium silicate thin films are characterized through a multitude of analytical probing tools, i.e., capacitance-voltage measurements, current-voltage characteristics, spectroscopic ellipsometry, scanning electron microscopy, atomic force microscopy, and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy. The adsorption mechanism of the precursor on a substrate surface is analyzed using density functional theory calculations. The physical thickness and surface morphology of the thin films were observed via high-resolution field-emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM, S-4800, Hitachi, Japan). Atomic force microscopy (AFM, XE-100, Park System, Korea) was employed to examine the surface morphology of the thin films. High-precision X-ray diffraction (XRD, D-2000, Rikagu, Japan) was used to determine the crystallinity of the thin films. After preliminary Ar plasma etching for 30 sec, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (Thermo Fisher Scientific, K-alpha, Rockford, Il, USA) was used to obtain the chemical states of elements, compositions and trace element amounts of the films. DFT calculations were performed using Gaussian 16 program suite."
