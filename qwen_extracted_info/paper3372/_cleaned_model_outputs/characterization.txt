  "evidence": "The crystallinities of the IGZO thin films were revealed by X-ray diffraction (XRD) (λ = 1.5406 Å) with the 2-theta method. Chemical bond states were analyzed by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) (Versaprobe II), which was carried out using a monochromatic Al Kα (1486.6 eV) source with a pass energy of 20 eV. Film thickness and roughness were evaluated using a grazing-incidence X-ray reflectivity instrument (GIXRR, SmartLab RIGAKU). Optical properties were investigated using an UV-visible spectrophotometer (Hitachi UH4150). Temperature dependent electrical properties such as carrier density, hall mobility, and resistivity were investigated by AC-field Hall effect measurement (ResiTest8400, Toyo Corp.) with the van der Pauw configuration. The microstructure of the IGZO thin films were investigated using TEM (JEM-2010F, JEOL)."
