  "evidence": "The structural properties of the films are investigated by diffraction using a transmission electron microscope (JEOL FE). Compositional analysis is performed by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, MgKα source), energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS, Zeiss LEO 1560), secondary ion mass spectrometry (SIMS, Cameca Wf), and Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS, NEC 3UH Pelletron). The resulting film thicknesses are determined by spectroscopic ellipsometry (J.A. Woollam M-2000UI) and confirmed by cross-sectional scanning electron microscopy (SEM, Zeiss LEO 1560). Electrical measurements of the films are carried out in a nitrogen-filled glove box using a Keithley 2400 SourceMeter. The optical absorption characteristics of the films are measured with a UV−VIS spectrometer (PerkinElmer Lambda 950)."
