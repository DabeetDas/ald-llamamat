  "evidence": "We show that a dynamically stable Ga film is formed on (0001) AlN in a large range of Ga fluxes at a substrate temperature of 740 °C. This feature allows for atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) of GaN on AlN by alternate exposure to Ga and N flux. The self-regulation of the Ga adsorption leads to an amount of 1.4 ± 0.3 MLs of GaN deposition per ALE cycle. TEM, AFM, and CL characterizations have demonstrated the potentiality of the ALE technique for the realization of GaN/AlN quantum well heterostructures with uniform and reproducible quantum well thickness as well as quantum dots for optoelectronic applications in the ultraviolet spectral range."
