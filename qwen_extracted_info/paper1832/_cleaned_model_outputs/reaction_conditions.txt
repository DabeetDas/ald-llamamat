For this reason, it was not surprising that the homogeneous particles of SiO2-IL-Ti(salen), which gave less stable emulsions, afforded poor conversion of methyl phenyl sulfide (71%) with lower chemoselectivity (80%) and ee value (82%) under identical conditions (Table 1, entry 5). The smaller emulsion droplets formed were more favorable for the transformation (Table 1, entries 1–4). This tendency was probably due to an increased interfacial contact area, which was governed by emulsion droplet size. A high interphase contact area compromised diffusion resistance at the oil–water interface and led to an enhancement in the catalysis efficiency."
