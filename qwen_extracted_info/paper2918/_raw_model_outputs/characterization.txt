  "evidence": "For X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis a Siemens D 5000 diffractometer was used. The geometries used were u-2u scans for phase identification, rocking curve (v scans) measurements to obtain full width at half maximum (FWHM) values, and w scans to obtain the in-plane orientations for epitaxially grown films. The XRD patterns of the titanium oxide films grown on soda-lime glass substrates at different deposition temperatures are shown in Fig . 1. The rocking curve FWHM values plotted vs. the deposition temperatures, for the (d) rutile 1 0 1 reflection of films grown on a-Al2O3 (0 1 2) substrate, and (j) 2 0 0 anatase reflection of films grown on MgO (0 0 1) substrates. The occurrence of six peaks in the w scan for the 1 1 9 reflection of the substrate is expected since the (0 0 1) surface has six-fold symmetry. The rocking curve FWHM values for the anatase 2 0 0 reflection are plotted against the deposition temperature in Fig. 3. It can be seen that the rocking curve FWHM values generally decrease with increasing deposition temperature, with the smallest value, 1.29 8, obtained for the film deposited at 3758C. The w scan measurements were carried out and the two w scans for the anatase 3 0 1 and the MgO 0 2 4 reflections are shown in Fig. 12. The occurrence of four peaks in the w scan for the anatase 3 0 1 reflection needs an explanation, since the corresponding (3 0 1) plane has two-fold symmetry toward the (2 0 0) plane.
