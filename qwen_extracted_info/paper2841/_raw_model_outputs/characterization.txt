  "evidence": "The displacement and water content of IPMCs are measured by a laser displacement sensor (Keyence LK-G80) and electronic balance (SHIMADZU AUW-120D), respectively. The electrical current and the electrical potential can be measured by the two multimeters with series and parallel connections, respectively. The function generator and the two multimeters are connected to a computer through a GPIB-USB-HS wire (National Instrument). The electronic balance and the laser displacement sensor are also connected to the computer through an RS232 interface. These fine instruments are controlled to acquire data simultaneously using a LabVIEW program. The water content measured at 500 mV s−1 using a cyclic voltammogram ranged from 85.9% to 100%."
