  "evidence": "These films and interface structures are probed using Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy along with Z-contrast imaging and electron energy loss spectroscopy (EELS) of a scanning transmission electron microscope. The interface structure of HfO2/Si(1 0 0) is further investigated using angle resolved X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy to probe the core level orbitals (Hf 4f, Si 2p, O 1s) at high resolution. The XPS studies show that the binding energies remain unchanged with film depth and that there is no apparent signature of silicate structure in the as-deposited films. EELS spectra taken at the interface and XPS measurements suggest the interface is mainly silicon oxide. The HfO2 films (2–15 nm thick) were deposited at 300 /C176 C and characterized with optical reflectometry and ellipsometry (J.A. Woollam Co., Inc., Model M-44) for film thickness. Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (Nicolet, Magna-IR 560) was used in the normal transmission mode over the wavenumber range of 4000–400 cm /C01. STEM/EELS and TEM (JEOL 2010F) were used for film structure studies. The combination of Z-contrast imaging (which can identify small changes in atomic number) and EELS (which can quantify composition and bonding fluctuations) provides accurate description of the structure and chemistry of the interface with high spatial resolution (<2 A˚)."
