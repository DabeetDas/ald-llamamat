  "evidence": "The crystallite orientations of Sc2O3 films were determined by X-ray diffraction using Cu KR radiation in a Philips MPD 1880 instrument. AFM measurements were carried out by a Nanoscope III atomic force microscope (Digital Instruments) operated in tapping mode. Elemental concentrations were determined by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) at the Accelerator Laboratory of the University of Helsinki. Infrared bands at 425 and 630 cm-1 were observed regardless of the precursor used or the deposition temperature. These bands can be identified as being due to Sc2O3."
