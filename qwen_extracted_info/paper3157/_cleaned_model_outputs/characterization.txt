  "evidence": "Angle-resolved X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (ARXPS) was performed in a Theta300 system (Thermo Instruments) in parallel angle-resolved mode using monochromatized Al Kα radiation. These X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) measurements were performed ex situ, but air exposure between germanium oxidation and loading the samples in the XPS tool was limited to less than 15 min. Time-of-Flight Secondary-Ion Mass Spectroscopy (TOFSIMS) was used for depth profiling, using a dual-ion-beam setup with a 500 eV Xe+ ion beam. Spectroscopic ellipsometry was used to verify oxide thicknesses. The electrical characteristics were measured with a Keithley K4200 semiconductor parameter analyzer and an HP4284 LCR meter. The EOT was extracted by fitting the capacitance–voltage (C-V) curves using the Hauser routine, including quantum mechanical corrections. The XPS Ge 3d peak was analyzed to determine the oxidation states of Ge in the oxide."
