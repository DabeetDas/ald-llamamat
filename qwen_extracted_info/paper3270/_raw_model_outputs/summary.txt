  "evidence": "The results indicate that the process window is 125–225 °C with a growth rate of ~ 0.073 Å/cycle. The deposited films contain N and O elements besides dominant Co and C. Furthermore, the prolongation of the NH3 pulse time significantly enhances the conductivity of the Co film and a low resistivity of 117 μΩ cm can be achieved with a NH3 pulse time of 40 s. The root mean square roughness shows a smaller variation with the deposition temperature and maintains a low value of ~ 0.3 nm, indicative of a flat Co film."
