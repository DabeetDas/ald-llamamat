  "evidence": "We demonstrate that to control a preferential growth mode one has to correlate growth temperature and separation time (so-called purging time) between pulses of precursors. We have demonstrated recently that this enables to obtain ZnMnO films free of foreign phases and free of metallic inclusions. This is also a very efficient precursor for ZnO growth, as we discuss separately in the forthcoming publication."
