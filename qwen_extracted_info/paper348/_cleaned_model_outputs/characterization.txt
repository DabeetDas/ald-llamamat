  "characterization_methods": ["Mass spectrometry"],
  "evidence": "Surface reactions in ALE were studied with quadrupole mass spectrometer when growing polycrystalline ZnS from ZnCI2 and H2S. The desorption of ZnCI2 was found to affect the growth most. On the other hand, the growth is relatively insensitive to reactant dose. Real time mass spectrometry is shown to be a powerful measurement technique for studying surface dynamics, because the step by step growth in ALE separates the reaction phases from each other."
