  "evidence": "The films were found to be insulating with a dielectric constant (ε) typically approximately 8. The topography of films deposited on soda-lime glass with 5000 pulse cycles at 200–350 8C with and without sequential CO pulses was analyzed with AFM. The films were examined both as deposited and calcined with a Siemens D5000 diffractometer equipped with a Gobel mirror to obtain parallel Cu Kα radiation. This set-up was used both for thickness measurements with X-ray profilometry and conventional u–2u X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis in reflection mode and reciprocal mapping. Selected films were analyzed with transmission FT-IR spectroscopy. Impedance measurements were performed in ambient air at 25 8C with a HP 4192A impedance analyzer. Rocking curve analyses were performed for the (006) peak for all films deposited at 250 8C and films deposited on a-Al2O3 (001) at 350 8C as well as (104) peaks for films deposited at 350 8C. Reciprocal space mapping of CaCO3 was performed for two of the (1,0,10) peaks separated by 608 in the w-scan. The XRD analyses of the samples were unable to detect traces of either the aragonite- or vaterite-type variants."
