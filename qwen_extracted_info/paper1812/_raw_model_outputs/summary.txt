  "evidence": "Titanium phosphate thin films were deposited by a new plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition process. The process consisted of sequential exposures to trimethyl phosphate (TMP) plasma, O2 plasma and titanium isopropoxide (TTIP) vapor. At a substrate temperature of 300 ◦C and using saturated pulse times, linear growth with a GPC of 0.66 nm/cycle and without nucleation delay was achieved. The as-deposited films were amorphous, while crystalline TiP2O7 was formed upon annealing in air or helium atmospheres. In lithium-ion test cells, the as-deposited films showed insertion and extraction of Li+ around a potential of 2.7 V vs. Li/Li+."
