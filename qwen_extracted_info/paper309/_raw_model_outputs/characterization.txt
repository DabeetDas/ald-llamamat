Agglomeration and aggregation of nanoparticles caused by ALD of the oxidizers are investigated by laser diffraction particle size analyzer and specific surface area analysis. Combustion properties of the nanothermites are probed by laser ignition technique. The amount of ZnO or SnO2 deposited on the Al powder was determined by sample weight gain measured with an analytical balance. Surface compositions of the nanothermites were detected by an X-ray photoelectron spectrometer. Sizes, shapes, and structures of the nanothermites were characterized by a field emission scanning electron microscope equipped with energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy and a high-resolution transmission electron microscope. Crystal structures of the nanothermites were measured with a Rigaku D/Max 2400 X-ray diffractometer using copper K-α radiation. Specific surface areas of the nanopowders were measured by a Micromeritic ASAP2020 physisorption analyzer using nitrogen adsorption isotherm. Particle size distributions were measured by a Malvern Mastersize2000 laser diffraction particle size analyzer."
