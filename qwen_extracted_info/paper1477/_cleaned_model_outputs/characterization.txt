  "evidence": "The thicknesses of the films deposited on Si(100)-substrates were measured with energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS). EDS measurements were performed with an Oxford INCA 350 Energy spectrometer connected to a Hitachi S-4800 field emission scanning electron microscope (FESEM). The morphologies of the films were studied with FESEM and atomic force microscopy (AFM) (Nanoscope V, Veeco Instruments). The crystal structure of the film was analyzed by X-ray diffraction (XRD) with a PANalytical /C19XPert PRO MPD X-ray diffractometer. Resistivities of the films were measured with a four-point probe (Keithley 2400 SourceMeter with a Cascade Microtech four-point probe). Compositions of selected silver films were analyzed with time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) and Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS)."
