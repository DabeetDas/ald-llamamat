  "evidence": "Ruthenium (Ru) thin films were grown via atomic layer deposition (ALD) using a novel Ru precursor with enhanced reactivity, namely Ru(chd)2 and O2. Self-limiting growth during the Ru ALD process was achieved by varying the Ru precursor and O2 feeding times. Metallic Ru films with a low resistivity (10–16 mO cm) grew at deposition temperatures between 200 and 300 1C, where the growth per cycle (GPC) during Ru ALD was 0.2 to 0.4 Å cy /C01 at 265 1C. The Ru incubation times were considerably shorter using the novel precursor (negligible on Pt and TiN, B22 cycles on SiO2) compared with those associated with Ru ALD using a high-valency Ru precursor and O2."
