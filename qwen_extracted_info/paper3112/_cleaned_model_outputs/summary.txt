  "evidence": "The study evaluated several precursors such as tetrakis(dimethylamino) titanium (TDMAT), tetrakis(diethylamino) titanium (TDEAT), tetrakis(ethylmethylamino) titanium (TEMAT) along with novel PrimeTi TM, StarTi TM and TyALDTM for TiO2 ALD application with both water and ozone as the oxidizer. Among the amino-compounds, TyALDTM has an improved thermal stability of 50 °C compared to the standard solution (TDMAT, TDEAT and TEMAT) while having the same order of deposition rate. Among all the existing solutions, StarTi TM is very promising. The demonstration of an ALD regime up to 400 °C is very attractive."
