Films are deposited at 200 °C from tetrakis dimethylamido titanium and H2O. Transmission electron microscopy data show that the starting surface consists of ~2.6 nm of native oxide and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy indicates a gradual reduction in the thickness of the oxide layer as the thickness of the TiO2 film increases. Approximately 0.1 –0.2 nm of arsenic and gallium suboxide is detected at the interface after 250 process cycles."
