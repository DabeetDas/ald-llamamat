The film growth mechanism was studied by quartz crystal microbalance in the temperature range 200-350 °C. The reactivity of O2 toward this layer was sufficient to form TiO2 at an O2 pulse duration of 2 s when the substrate temperature was not lower than 235 °C. TiO2 films were also grown on Si(100) substrates at deposition temperatures between 230 and 460 °C. No residual iodine could be detected in the films grown at temperatures higher than 230 °C. Phase-pure anatase was formed in the whole temperature range except at the highest temperature where rutile was also obtained."
