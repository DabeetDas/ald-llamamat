  "evidence": "The effects of B2H6 pretreatment on ALD of W film using a sequential supply of WF6 and SiH4 on thermally grown SiO2 and TiN films at 300°C were investigated. The results show that the B2H6 pretreatment reduces the incubation time for film growth. X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy and scanning electron microscopy analysis suggest that elemental B on SiO2, released during B2H6 pretreatment, induce rapid W nucleation. The drastically improved step coverage of ALD-W film is achieved at the ultrahigh aspect ratio contact by the enhanced nucleation and growth via B2H6 pretreatment."
