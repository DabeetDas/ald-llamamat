 "evidence": "The structural properties of ZnO films were characterized by x-ray diffraction (XRD) in a full angular range using the X’Pert MPD diffractometer. The sample surface morphology was examined by an atomic force microscope (AFM). Electrical properties were obtained from Hall measurements at room temperature. In PL research, the samples were detected by the ANDOR SE303i-B spectrometer and the ANDOR DV42 OA-OE CCD camera. The equipment used for Hall effect experiments was the RH2035 system produced by PhysTech GmbH. From EDX investigation (not discussed here), we found that the free electron concentration does not correlate with the hydrogen concentration in studied samples."
