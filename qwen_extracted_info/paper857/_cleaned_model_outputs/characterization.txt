  "evidence": "Electrical and corrosion properties of PEALD-TiAlN were systematically investigated and characterized by X-ray diffraction (XRD), field-emission electron microscopy (FESEM), contact-mode atomic force microscopy (AFM), and electrochemical analysis. Furthermore, these properties were compared to those of TiN films to clarify the effects of AlN addition on the corrosion protection of SS316L. The films’ crystal structures were investigated by X-ray diffractometer (XRD, Bruker, D8 Advance), and their thickness and surface morphology were analyzed by field-emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM, Hitachi S-4800). Composition of the TiAlN thin films was analyzed by energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX). Contact-mode atomic force microscopy (AFM) was performed to investigate the surface roughness of the TiN and TiAlN thin films. The corrosion resistance of TiN- and TiAlN-coated SS316L was analyzed using an electrochemical analyzer (Princeton, Applied Research, VersaSTAT-4)."
