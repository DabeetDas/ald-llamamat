 "evidence": "We also characterized the initial surface of the substrate before the ALD cycles by means of ultra high vacuum (UHV) atomic force microscopy (AFM). XPS is a very powerful tool technique for investigating interfaces, as one can get important chemical and physical information over a region of a few nanometers underneath the thin film surface. AFM delivers the local topographical information of the substrate. The main advantage of using the UHV-AFM system is the ability to control the surface perfection of the initial substrate and to investigate clean and artifact-free surfaces."
