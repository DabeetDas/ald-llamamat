  "evidence": "The Si2p spectrum has various components deriving from the substrate (at about 99.5 eV), from the non-stoichiometric Si-oxide (100–102.5 eV), and from SiO2 (at about 103.5 eV). These peaks change in binding energy and intensity when the ALD of HfO2 proceeds. We found the values 2.2, 3.2 and 4.7 for Sample A (etched with the 0.5% HF solution) before and after 3 and 6 ALD cycles (corresponding to 1 and 2 HfO2 layers) had been performed, respectively. For Sample B, the ratios calculated on the substrate, and after 9 and 18 ALD cycles (corresponding to 1 and 2 HfO2 layers) were 0.7, 3, and 7 respectively. From the values of these ratios, initial SiO2 thicknesses of about 0.5 nm for Sample A and of only 0.2 nm for Sample B were determined. After the growth of 1 HfO2 layer, we found an interfacial SiO2 layer of about 0.6 nm on both samples. After the growth of the second HfO2 layer the interfacial SiO2 was 0.75 nm in Sample A and 0.9 nm for Sample B."
