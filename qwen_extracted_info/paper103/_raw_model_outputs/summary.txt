  "evidence": "The mechanism of atomic layer epitaxy (ALE) of cadmium telluride has been studied. Auger electron spectroscopy is used to measure the isothermal re-evaporation rates of elemental Cd and Te deposits on the (111)A and (111)B surfaces of CdTe substrates. The results include an observation that the sticking coefficients of Cd and Te are smaller than unity at the growth temperatures typical of CdTe ALE. The activation energies for reevaporation of the near-interface layer region are estimated to be: 1.5 eV for Te on the (111)A face, 1.0 eV for Te on (111)B and 0.5 eV for Cd on (111)B."
