  "evidence": "Thin ﬁlms and nanotubes of iron oxide are deposited using atomic layer deposition (ALD) on Si(100) and anodic aluminum oxide (AAO), respectively. Ferrocene, Fe(Cp)2, and oxygen are used as precursors. Successful depositions are carried out in the temperature range 350–500 -C on Si(100), while all depositions on AAO are made at 400 -C. The growth per cycle values are around 0.14 nm on Si(100) in the temperature range 350–500 -C and 0.06 nm on AAO. Below 500 -C, the iron oxide crystallizes as a phase mixture on both types of substrates. One of the phases is identiﬁed as the rhombohedral Fe 2O3 phase (hematite), but the second phase cannot be unambiguously identiﬁed. Above 500 -C, only phase pure hematite is detected. For deposition of nanotubes, in-house made AAO membranes are used, having an aspect ratio of 30."
