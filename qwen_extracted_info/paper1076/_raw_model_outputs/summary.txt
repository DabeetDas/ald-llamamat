  "evidence": "Tungsten (W) atomic layer deposition (ALD) was investigated on a variety of polymer films and polymer particles. These polymers included polyethylene, polyvinyl chloride, polystyrene, polycarbonate, polypropylene and polymethylmethacrylate. The W ALD was performed at 80 °C using WF6 and Si2H6 as the gas phase reactants. W ALD on flat polymer films can eventually nucleate and grow after more than 60 AB cycles. The W ALD films displayed an excellent, mirror-like optical reflectivity. The resistivity was 100 –400 µΩ cm for W ALD films with thicknesses from 95 –845 Å. W ALD was also observed on polymer particles after W ALD in a rotary reactor. The nucleation of W ALD on polymers without the Al2O3 ALD seed layer required N50 AB cycles. In contrast, the polymer particles treated with only 5 AB cycles of Al2O3 ALD were observed to blacken after 25 AB cycles of W ALD. W ALD on polymers may have applications for flexible optical mirrors, electromagnetic interference shielding and gas diffusion barriers."
