  "evidence": "We have grown HfO2 on Si(001) by atomic layer deposition using HfCl4 and H2O as precursors. The early stages of the ALD were investigated with high-resolution photoelectron spectroscopy and x-ray absorption spectroscopy. The first layer consists of a sparse and Cl-contaminated oxide because of the incomplete oxidation, and the second layer is denser than the first one and with an almost stoichiometric O/Hf ratio. The substrate temperature during the growth was set to 300 °C because it was demonstrated that the best substrate temperature for delivering high quality HfO2 films using HfCl4 and H2O is between 300 and 350 °C."
