  "evidence": "ALD has been investigated for the synthesis of CGO ultrathin layers. Film deposition was studied in a commercial flow type F-120 ALD reactor (ASM Micro-chemistry, Finland) using nitrogen as a carrier and purging gas. Several substrates were used including LSM doped with 20% of strontium, Ni-YSZ, being the most often used anode material, as well as silicon Si(100). All ALD depositions were performed using ~-diketonate-type precursors, viz. Ce(thd)3 and Gd(thd)3, synthesized by previously reported methods. Ozone generated from oxygen gas (99.999%) in an ozone generator (Fischer model 502) was used as oxygen source. The film deposition took place at reduced pressure of 2-3 mbar and the reaction chamber temperature was held at 250 °C."
