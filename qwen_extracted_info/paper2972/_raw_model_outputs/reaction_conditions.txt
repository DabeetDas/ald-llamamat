  "evidence": "Yu et al. reported a comparison between the tendencies to form silicates, which have lower dielectric constants, of HfO2 and Al2O3. They reported a variation in the ratio of the oxygen to silicon bond (O-Si) to the silicon to silicon (Si-Si) bond using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) analysis as the Hf to Al concentration ratio varies in the HfxAlyOz films, which were grown on Si by atomic layer deposition (ALD). They reported that the O-Si bonding increased as the annealing temperature increased up to 1000°C, irrespective of the film composition. However, pure Al2O3 and HfO2 films showed the smallest and largest increases, respectively, suggesting that Al2O3 has less tendency to form silicates than HfO2. The authors also reported that the Al2O3 interlayer interposed between HfO2 film and the Si substrate made the dielectric film stack more thermally stable against a decrease in the capacitance density than a single-layer HfO2 film due to less Si diffusion during the postannealing process."
