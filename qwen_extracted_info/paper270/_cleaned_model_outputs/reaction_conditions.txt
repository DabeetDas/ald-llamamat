  "evidence": "The surface morphology of VALE grown GaAs is mirror-like and no gallium droplets are found even for long TMGa exposure times or high exposure levels. These observations imply that homogeneous decomposition of TMGa is nonexistent, and that surface reactions are the dominant processes in VALE. The mechanism of the self-limiting decomposition of TBAs is probably due to the low sticking coefficient of As on As-rich surfaces. Background As species are always present during VALE growth. We believe that the species are As2 or As4 which come from the deposits near the heater assembly. The background As species hit the substrate surface continuously and can be considered as an unintentional As source in our chamber. Since the sticking coefficient of As2 or As4 on As-rich surface is close to zero, there is no reaction during the TBAs exposure cycle. However, during the TMGa exposure cycle, the background As species can react with TMGa and cause more than monolayer growth."
