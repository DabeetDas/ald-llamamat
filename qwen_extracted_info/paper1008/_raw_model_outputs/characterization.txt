The cross-sections of the layers were studied by scanning electron microscopy (SEM) using a LEO 1550 microscope. X-ray photo-electron spectroscopy (XPS, PHI Quantum 2000 XPS system) was used to detect residues of nitrogen, carbon and oxygen in the annealed layers. Surface roughness was measured by Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM, PSIA XEI 150 equipment)."
