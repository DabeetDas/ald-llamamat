Iron nanoparticles were produced by thermal decomposition of cryogenically milled iron oxalate. A titanium dioxide (TiO2) thin film was deposited on the iron nanoparticles using ALD at 100 °C with TiCl4 and H2O2 as precursors. However, due to the high surface area, the iron nanoparticles were unstable and oxidized during the TiO2 ALD process. To address this, an aluminum nitride (AlN) film was deposited in situ at 250 °C using TMA and NH3 as precursors to passivate the iron core particles. The AlN film significantly reduced iron oxidation, preserving the magnetic moment and enhancing the photoactivity of the TiO2 film.
