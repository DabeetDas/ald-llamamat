 "evidence": "The silver precursor used is synthesized in-house, and characterized using CHN elemental analysis, infrared (IR) spectroscopy , nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), mass spectrometry (MS), and thermogravimetric analysis/single differential thermal analysis (TGA/SDTA). The films were polycrystalline according to X-ray diffraction (XRD), and exhibited (111) and (200) reflections. The films were very rough as analyzed by X-ray reflectivity (XRR). The film composition was studied with TOF-ERDA. The films were successfully deposited on silicon and glass. The film crystallinity was studied with grazing incidence (GI) XRD. Film densities were measured using XRR. Film thicknesses were studied with energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX). The film conformality was studied with the Hitachi S-4800 FESEM from samples deposited onto a substrate with patterned trenches."
