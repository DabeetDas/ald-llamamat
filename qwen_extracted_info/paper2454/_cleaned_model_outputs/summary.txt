  "evidence": "In this work, we synthesized large-scale and thickness-controllable MoS2 films on sapphire substrate by ALD at 150 oC with molybdenum hexcarbonyl and hexamethyldisilathiane (HMDST) as precursors followed by high-temperature annealing in sulfur atmosphere.
