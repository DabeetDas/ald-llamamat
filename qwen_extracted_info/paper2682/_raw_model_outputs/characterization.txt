  "evidence": "Rutherford backscattering (RBS) was measured at the RUBION (University of Bochum, Germany), using a dynamitron tandem accelerator with a 2 MeV 4 He+ beam. For detecting low-weight atoms (like carbon and oxygen), nuclear reaction analysis (NRA) with a 1 MeV 2H+ ion beam was used. Middle infrared (MIR) spectroscopic ellipsometric measurements were carried out using the SENDIRA ellipsometer (SENTECH Instruments GmbH, Berlin, Germany). It is equipped with an FTIR spectrometer as light source (Varian 640-IR) and a deuterated triglycine sulfate detector. The MIR spectra were taken at an angle of incidence of U = 65° in the spectral range from 400 to 6000 cm-1. Ellipsometry spectra between the UV and near-infrared (190 to 2500 nm) were measured using the SENresearch 4.0 SER850 DUV (SENTECH Instruments GmbH, Berlin, Germany) with an angle of incidence of U = 65°. The electrical film properties were determined using the van der Pauw method as well as the Hall technique for measuring carrier density and mobility. The WVTR was determined by an optical Ca test on 70×70 mm glass substrates, where the change in optical transmission has been related to the amount of calcium which has been corroded to CaOH in a certain time. The Ca-tests were carried out in a climate cabinet. The measurement technique is described in more detail in our previous work. Atomic force microscopy (Fig. S2) showed a similar surface morphology for all our layers with a roughness of about 0.3 nm (rms)."
