  "characterization_methods": ["Quadrupole Mass Spectrometer (QMS)", "Quartz Crystal Microbalance (QCM)"],
  "evidence": "The reaction mechanisms in the atomic layer deposition of ZrO2 were studied in situ with a quadrupole mass spectrometer (QMS) and a quartz crystal microbalance (QCM). The quadrupole mass spectrometer allows us to monitor the gas phase of the reactor and especially the growth reaction byproducts, the amounts of which give direct information concerning the reaction mechanism. The quartz crystal microbalance gives the relative changes of the mass of the film, which give information on the mechanism when one compares the mass change over a complete ALD cycle to the mass changes during each step of the process."
