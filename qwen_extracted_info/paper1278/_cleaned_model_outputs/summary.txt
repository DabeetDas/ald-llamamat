  "evidence": "The ALD of NiS has been demonstrated with great promise for electrocatalytic water splitting applications. The ALD process used bis(N,N’-di-tert-butylacetamidinato)nickel(II) (Ni(amd)2) as the nickel precursor and H2S gas as the sulfur source. Previously, this process was found to follow a typical layer-by-layer ALD growth mode and was able to produce fairly smooth, pure, and conformal NiS films at deposition temperature ranging from 90−200 °C. The growth rate of NiS was found fairly low (~0.015 nm/cycle), which implies that the surface reactions perhaps deviated from the conventional ligand-exchange scheme."
