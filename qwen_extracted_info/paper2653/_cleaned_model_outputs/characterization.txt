  "evidence": "X-ray diffraction of the as-deposited films indicated that they were amorphous. Atomic force microscopy of 35–36 nm thick manganese borate films grown within the 300–350 /C14 C ALD window showed root mean square surface roughnesses of 0.4–0.6 nm. Film stoichiometries were assessed by x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy and time of flight-elastic recoil detection analysis. Film thicknesses were measured by cross-sectional scanning electron microscopy (SEM). UV-Vis analyses were performed on a JASCO V-570 UV/VIS/NIR spectrophotometer. Thermogravimetric (TGA) and differential thermal (DTA) analyses were conducted on a Perkin-Elmer Pyris 1 TGA system."
