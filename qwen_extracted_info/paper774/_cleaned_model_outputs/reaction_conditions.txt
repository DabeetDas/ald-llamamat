  "evidence": "The surface reactions for both Th-ALD and PEALD MgO films involve the self-limited chemisorption of the Mg precursor and co-reactant. The Mg(EtCp)2 precursor is chemisorbed onto the surface, followed by a purge with N2, then the co-reactant (H2O or O2 plasma) is introduced. The self-limited surface reactions are observed for both ALD processes, indicating the formation of Mg-O bonds and the regeneration of surface hydroxyl groups. The chemical binding states of the Th-ALD and PEALD MgO films grown at various temperatures from 200 to 400 °C were investigated through XPS analysis. For all MgO samples, the Mg 2p subpeaks at 51.5 eV and O 1s subpeaks at 531.9 eV are observed predominantly, suggesting the formation of Mg–O lattice bonds. Furthermore, small subpeaks at 52.4 and 534 eV from Mg 2p and O 1s are observed from all MgO films, which indicates the formation of Mg(OH)2. The observation of Mg–OH bonds formed by the MgO films is consistent with the previously reported MgO films grown by non-vacuum deposition processes such as sol-gel method."
