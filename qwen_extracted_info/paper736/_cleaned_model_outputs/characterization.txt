  "evidence": "Field-emission scanning electron microscope, JEOL, JSM 6340F was used to investigate the surface morphology. Dual Beam Focus Ion Beam (FIB) was used to observe the inner morphology of TiO2/MoS2-IO. Energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS) was used to perform the elemental analysis. Raman spectra were collected on Renishaw inVia Raman system with 532 nm as excitation wavelength, and a 50 × objective lens was used in our measurements. X-ray diffraction pattern (XRD) was measured using Bruker D8 x-ray diffractometer, equipped with Cu K/H9251 radiation source. UV–vis diffuse reflectance spectra were measured using Varian, Cary 5000. The MoS2 film thickness on the Si/SiO2 (SiO2:280 nm) substrate was measured using a spectroscopic ellipsometry (J. A. Woollam)."
