 "evidence": "Using these results, we demonstrate the possibility of growing tilted superlattices made out of CdTe and MnTe. However, the lateral ordering created by this structure is strongly limited by two effects, the Cd/Mn atomic exchange and the step array disorder. The TSLs were not visible in high resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM) although ordinary superlattices are well imaged. This shows that the chemical composition difference is not large. Consequently, (002) dark field imaging was employed to study the TSL. The image of each step is fuzzy, indicating that steps are not well aligned along the A-direction. In addition, recent observations of CdTe vicinal surfaces by STM evidence the large meandering of k110l steps. The steps remain then parallel to the (100) direction as already demonstrated for CdTe by STM study, and are straight over several tens of nanometers. Note however that for MnTe, no preferential growth along k100l steps seems to occur as revealed by AFM images. A quantitative analysis of this intermixing was performed for both CdTe/CdMnTe interfaces and CdTe/MnTe ones. We took into account this intermixing effect in TSLs grown on vicinal surfaces by considering that the impinging atoms during growth are first exchanged with the last incorporated monolayer and then migrate to the step edge. The Mn concentration variation on the step terraces between 35% and 65% instead of alternated pure CdTe and MnTe. Such an approach was used successfully to explain quantitatively the low lateral potential modulation in the case of GaAs/AlAs TSLs."
