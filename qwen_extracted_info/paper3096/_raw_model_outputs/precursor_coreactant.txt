  "evidence": "The precursor requires sufficient vapor pressure for efficient mass transport onto the substrate surface while maintaining good thermal stability and surface adsorption affinity under ALD reaction conditions. In this work the applicability of a precursor, bis(diethylamino) silane, SiH2N(C2H5)2 (BDEAS), is studied for the ALD of HfSiOx, with tetrakis(diethylamino)hafnium (TDEAH) as the source of Hf and O3 as the oxidizer. The ALD process temperature windows for the deposition of silicon dioxide and hafnium oxide from the aforementioned precursors overlap between 200 and 250°C. The same O3 pulsing parameters were used for ALD of SiO2, HfO2, and HfSiOx. The self-limiting nature of the ALD process was also indicated by the high degree of uniformity in the film thickness across the substrate surface. The optimal length of subsequent nitrogen purge was determined to be 5 s."
