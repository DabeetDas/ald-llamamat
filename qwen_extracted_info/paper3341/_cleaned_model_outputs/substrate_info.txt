  "evidence": "Titanium alloy foils (Ti6Al4V , grade 5) were cut into 10 mm × 70 mm and 10 mm × 10 mm pieces. Obtained substrate samples were ultrasonically cleaned sequentially in acetone (15 min), 80% ethanol (5 min), and deionised water (15 min). The surface of the substrates used in the thermal oxidation process was activated by its immersion in a 1:1 mixture of 36.5% HCl and water at 80 ◦C for 30 min. After the activation, the samples were cleaned with deionised water and dried in Argon stream."
