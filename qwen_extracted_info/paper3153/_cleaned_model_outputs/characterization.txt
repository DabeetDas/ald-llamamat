  "evidence": "The film thickness was measured by ellipsometry and confirmed by high resolution transmission electron microscopy. The areal density of Ti ions in the films was measured by X-ray fluorescence (XRF) spectroscopy, which has an error of ~1%. The areal density measured by XRF was calibrated using a sample of which the Ti density was measured by Rutherford backscattering spectroscopy. X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was used to examine the chemical binding states. The film composition along the thickness direction and the residual carbon concentration were examined by Auger electron spectroscopy (AES). The surface morphology of the films was examined by atomic force microscopy (AFM, scan area of 2 μm). Glancing angle incidence X-ray diffraction (GAXRD) with an incidence angle of 1° was used to determine the crystalline quality and identify the phase of the TiO2 layers. The dielectric properties and leakage current properties of the films were measured using a Hewlett-Packard 4194 impedance analyzer at 10 kHz and a 4140B picoammeter, respectively."
