The purity and chemical composition of the thin films was investigated by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) using a Theta Probe (Thermo Scientific) instrument. Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and energy dispersive X- ray spectroscopy (EDX) were performed with a FEI Quanta 200F instrument."
