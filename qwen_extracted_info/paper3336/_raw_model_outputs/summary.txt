  "evidence": "Here we demonstrate a PE-ALD method with a processing temperature less than 250 °C, which produced plasmonic TiN thin films. The plasmon wavelengths of these films were found to be in the region of 500–600 nm and with low optical losses in that region after air exposure. We performed studies of thickness of 42 and 11 nm on MgO and Si (100). The 42-nm thick films on lattice-matched MgO substrates show a plasmon wavelength of 564 nm with a corresponding ε2 of 3.74, and ultrathin films even at thicknesses near 10 nm retain a plasmon resonance. The 11 nm thick films on lattice-matched MgO substrates show a plasmon wavelength of 613 nm with a corresponding ε2 of 4.5. Similarly, thick films on Si (100) substrates show a plasmon wavelength of 586 nm with a comparable ε2 of 4.21. Importantly, we introduced a hydrogen-plasma post-deposition treatment to further alter the optical properties of TiN while maintaining the thermal budget at the low temperature of 250 °C."
