  "evidence": "The trace element impurities of the films were determined semiquantitatively with Auger and X-ray fluorescence spectrometries. The chloride contents were chemically analysed using an indirect spectrophotometrical method. Film thicknesses were measured with a profilometer as well as spectrophotometrically. A scanning electron microscope (SEM) was used to determine the surface smoothness of the films. The presence of residual OH-groups in the films was studied using a Bruker IF S113v FTIR-spectrometer equipped with a specular reflectance accessory. The breakdown fields and the dielectric constants were measured from twenty five capacitors of each sample. The films have two types of Z-U behaviour. The dielectric constants of the films were approximately 8 but the films deposited at 773 K had slightly higher dielectric constants than films deposited at 673 K."
