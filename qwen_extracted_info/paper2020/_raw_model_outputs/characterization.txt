Therefore, the actual HfO2 film thicknesses were calibrated using cross-section high-resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM, JEOL JEM-3000F) equipped with a field-emission electron gun. A Hewlett-Packard 4194A impedance meter and a 4140B picoammeter were used for the capacitance–voltage (C–V) and current density–voltage (J–V) measurements, respectively.
