In situ XRD measurements were acquired during annealing of the films in a home-built heating chamber mounted on the diffractometer. The chemical composition of the deposited films was determined by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) using a Thermo VG Scientific ESCALAB 220i-XL with a monochromatic Al Kα X-ray source.
