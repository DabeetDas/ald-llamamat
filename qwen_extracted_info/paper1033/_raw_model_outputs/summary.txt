  "evidence": "Lanthanide oxide (Ln=Nd, Sm, Eu, Gd, Dy, Ho, Er and Tm) thin films were grown onto silicon (100) substrates by atomic layer deposition (ALD) using volatile h-diketonate-type Ln(thd)3 compounds and ozone as precursors. Nd2O3 was observed to contain hexagonal phase as well. Compositional studies by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) revealed the films to be nearly stoichiometric having carbon, fluorine and hydrogen as impurities. Electrical characterization showed that thin films were resistive having relative permittivities between 8.4 and 11.1."
