  "evidence": "In this study, we deposited hafnium–zirconium–oxide films on Si substrates by atomic layer deposition (ALD). The hafnium–zirconium–oxide film deposition was performed at 300°C. Zirconium tetrachloride (ZrCl4) and hafnium tetrachloride (HfCl4) were used as the metal precursors, and H2O was used as the oxidizing agent. Remote plasma nitridation (RPN) treatment successfully incorporated nitrogen atoms into the HfO2 and ZrO2 mixed-oxide films. The RPN treatment also caused the formation of a silicate layer in the interfacial region. The hafnium–zirconium–oxide films had partially crystallized structures; RPN treatment slightly retarded crystallization of the films after annealing. Films with RPN treatment showed a lower EOT, a lower leakage current, and a higher breakdown voltage than those without RPN treatment."
