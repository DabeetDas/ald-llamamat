X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) with a Fischerscope X-ray XDV-SDD was used to determine CIS thin film compositions. The electrical properties of solar cells were determined by current-voltage (I-V) characteristics measured under standard AM 1.5G spectrum (1000 W/m²) using a Newport Oriel Sol3A class AAA solar simulator. Quantum efficiency measurements were made with an Oriel IQE 200."
