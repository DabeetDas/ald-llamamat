  "evidence": "Successive reactions of gaseous trimethylaluminium (TMA) and ammonia on porous alumina were studied with the goal of finding suitable process conditions for preparing aluminium nitride (AlN) by atomic layer deposition (ALD). The reaction of TMA was studied at 353–623 K on alumina dehydroxylated at 473–1073 K, and the following reaction of ammonia at 423–823 K. Good temperatures for AlN deposition by ALD seem to be any temperature up to 573 K for the TMA reaction and 723 K or above for the ammonia reaction."
