X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) was used to investigate the stoichiometry and the elemental bonding states of the ﬁlms using Kratos AXIS-165. The Surface Analysis System was equipped with a monochromatic Al Kα (1486.6 eV) X-ray source and a concentric hemispherical analyzer coupled with a charge neutralizer operated at 15 kV and 10 mA. Surface morphology of 50 nm thick as-deposited Y2O3 ﬁlm was studied with a Nanoscope III atomic force microscope (Digital Instruments) operated in a contact mode at a scanning frequency of 4 Hz, and the root-mean-square (rms) roughness was determined. A high-resolution X-ray diffractometer (Philips X’pert) was used to obtain the crystal orientations of as-deposited Y2O3 ﬁlm. The X-ray source, operating at 40 mA and 45 kV, used a monochromatic Cu Kα radiation with angular divergence of 12 arc-s. Glancing incidence XRD (GIXRD) spectra were collected at the low angle of 0.7° to avoid interference from the single-crystal Si (100) substrate."
