 "evidence": "The thickness of the Ru films were determined by cross-sectional view transmission electron microscopy (XTEM, Tecnai F20 equipped with 200 kV accelerating voltage and field emission gun) analysis. Their resistivities were determined from the sheet resistances of the Ru films measured using a four-point probe and their thicknesses. Impurities in the Ru film, such as oxygen and carbon, were analyzed by secondary ion mass spectroscopy (SIMS, CAMECA IMS-6f in Korea Basic Science Institute) depth profiling. The film density was evaluated using X-ray reflectance (XRR, PANanalytical X’-pert MRD with Cu-K alpha radiation at 1.5 kW). X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis was performed for phase and crystallinity identification. Plan-view TEM was used to examine the microstructures of the Ru films. The step coverage of the ALD-Ru films was evaluated at contacts with different aspect ratios. The morphology of electroplated Cu was examined by cross-sectional view scanning electron microscopy (SEM)."
