The Ni atom in Ni(dmamb)2 precursor is bonded with two O atoms and two N atoms. So, Ni–O and Ni–N bondings in Ni(dmamb)2 are effectively reduced by both plasma reactants, and the N contamination is attributed to NH3 plasma reactant. The studies on the decomposition of NH3 in RF discharge have been many times reported, and the NH3 decomposes into several kinds of radicals, NHx and H3/C0x, where x is 1 or 2. For HPNi, the radicals in H2 plasma are H2 and H, so NHx radicals are additionally involved in the reaction chemistry of APNi to affect the increase of growth rate. The NHx radicals in NH3 plasma assists the reduction of the precursor rather than directly reduces the precursor."
