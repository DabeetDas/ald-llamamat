X-ray diffraction (XRD) measurements were performed by using high-sensitivity diffractometer (SmartLab, Rikagu, Japan, CuKα radiation, l = 1.5487 Å) in the 2u range of 10–80/C14. Electrical properties of the films were measured using a four-probe measuring device (HP 4155c, Agilent Technologies) and Hall measurement (HMS-3000, Ecopia). The film morphology was analyzed by using a field-emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM, S-4800, Hitachi). An energy dispersive spectrometer (EDS; 7593-H EMAX, Horiba) was utilized to analyze the elemental ratio in the film. The optical thicknesses of films were measured by using ellipsometer (FC-1, paultech). The current-voltage characteristics of DSCs were performed under the irradiation of standard simulated AM 1.5 illumination at 100 mW/cm2."
