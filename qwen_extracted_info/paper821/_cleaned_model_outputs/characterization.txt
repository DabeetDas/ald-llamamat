  "evidence": "Crystalline orientations and crystallinity of the deposited films were determined by X-ray diffraction with Cu Ka radiation in a Philips MPD 1880 diffractometer. Surface morphology was studied with Nanoscope III atomic force microscope (Digital Instruments) operated in tapping mode. Film compositions were measured by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) at the Accelerator Laboratory of the University of Helsinki. In order to get more information on residual carbon and hydrogen impurities, the films were also analyzed by FTIR spectroscopy. The electrical characterization, Gd2 O 3 films from Gd(thd)3/O3 or (CpCH3)3Gd/H2 O were deposited onto n- or p-type Si(100) substrates at 300 and 2501C, respectively. The capacitance–voltage (C–V) and current–voltage (I–V) measurements were carried out on Al/Gd2 O 3/native SiO2 /n- or pSi(100)/Al capacitor structures."
