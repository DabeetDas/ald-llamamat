  "evidence": "X-ray diffraction (XRD) and high-resolution transmission electron microscope (HRTEM) analyses evidenced that Co on Sb2Te3 grows preferentially oriented along the [00λ] direction, following the underlying rhombohedric substrate. Atomic force microscopy (AFM) measurements were performed at the Sb2Te3 and Co surfaces using a Bruker AFM commercial system. A four-circle X-ray diffractometer equipped with Cu Kα radiation was used for X-ray reflectivity (XRR), grazing incidence X-ray diffraction (GIXRD) and Bragg Brentano X-ray diffraction (BBXRD) measurements. The structural integrity and the layer thickness of the thickest samples (1,000 cycles) was checked by the scanning electron microscope (SEM) cross sections. Complementary information about the layer’s and the interface Co/Sb2Te3 structure and morphology, we employed cross sectional high resolution transmission electron microscopy (HRTEM) and energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS). HRTEM analyses clearly show how the Co layer adheres with a very high conformality to the underneath granular Sb2Te3 surface. The FFT of Pt is also shown that identifies its fcc crystal structure. The spatial distribution of the Co, Sb and Te elemental signals shows that their overlapping extends over a distance comparable with the surface roughness of the Sb2Te3 granular layer. The FFT analyses are reported for the highlighted region of the TEM image."
