  "evidence": "The surface morphologies of the nanomixed films were analyzed by an atomic force microscopy (AFM). The thickness of the films was confirmed by the cross-sectional images of transmission electron microscopy (TEM) and was well consistent with that obtained by controlling the number of cycles. The composition of HfxAlyOz films was controlled with a constant rule of 2 cycles of Hf and 1 cycle of Al until the nanomixed films have a desired thickness and was confirmed by a Rutherford backscattering spectroscopy (RBS). The capacitance–voltage characteristics of the nanomixed capacitors as a function of applied frequency are measured at various applied voltages using a Hewlett-Packard 4194A impedance analyzer. The dielectric constant and dissipation factor were measured at 100 kHz in capacitance vs. frequency data. Leakage current characteristics of the nanomixed capacitors was investigated by a Keithley 617 electrometer."
