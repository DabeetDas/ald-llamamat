  "evidence": "Electron-Enhanced Atomic Layer Deposition (EE-ALD) of Boron Nitride Thin Films at Room Temperature and 100 °C. The study describes the use of sequential exposures of borazine and electrons to deposit BN films at room temperature and 100 °C. The process utilizes electron-stimulated desorption (ESD) of hydrogen surface species to create reactive dangling bonds, facilitating borazine adsorption and reducing the temperature required for BN film deposition. The growth rate was found to be approximately 3.2 Å/cycle at electron energies of 80–160 eV. The films exhibited high purity, uniformity, and crystallinity, with the BN films being polycrystalline hexagonal and turbostratic BN. The study also demonstrated the potential of EE-ALD for bottom-up filling of trenches and vias in semiconductor fabrication."
