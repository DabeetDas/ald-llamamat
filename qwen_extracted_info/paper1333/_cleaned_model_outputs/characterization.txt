  "evidence": "In order to investigate the modification of the dielectric surface by the plasma treatment, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), attenuated total reflection infrared spectroscopy (ATR) and water contact angle measurements (WCA) were employed. The dielectric thickness loss after the plasma etch was measured by x-ray reflectivity (XRR). Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS) and depth profiling were used to quantify the Ru areal density and indiffusion into the pores when porous low-k films are used as substrates. In order to investigate and quantify the ELD Cu selectivity, top-down and cross-section scanning electron microscopy (SEM) combined with energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) elemetal quantification were exploited in this work."
