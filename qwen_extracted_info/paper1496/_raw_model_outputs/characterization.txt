The physical thickness of the STO films on Ru substrate was measured using an ellipsometer, and that of the STO films on RuO2 substrate was calculated by correlating the layer density and physical thickness of STO on Ru substrate. The impurity concentration in the films, including carbon, and the depth profile were examined by Auger electron spectroscopy (AES, Perkin-Elmer, PHI 660). The chemical binding states of the films were determined by X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, ThermoVG, SIGMA PROBE). The crystal structure of the film was investigated by glancing-angle mode X-ray diffraction using a Cu Kα X-ray source (GAXRD, PANalytical, X ’Pert Pro).
