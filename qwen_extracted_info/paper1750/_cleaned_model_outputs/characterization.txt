  "evidence": "The Bi/Ta ratio in the films was analyzed by a Link ISIS EDX spectrometer attached to a Zeiss DSM 962 SEM. Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS) and time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) measurements were performed using the 5 MV tandem accelerator EGP-10-II. A Bruker AXS D8 Advance diffractometer was used in the X-ray diffraction (XRD) measurements. Film thicknesses and refractive indices were determined by fitting optical transmittance and reflectance spectra measured within a wavelength range of 370–1100 nm."
