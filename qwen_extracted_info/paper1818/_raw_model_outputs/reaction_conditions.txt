The presence of –CO3 can be attributed to formation of rubidium carbonate from the 'excess' rubidium, i.e. the rubidium that cannot go into the TiOx matrix. This corresponds well with the stability levels that were observed for growth rate and composition as a function of the pulsed ratio of RbO tBu.
