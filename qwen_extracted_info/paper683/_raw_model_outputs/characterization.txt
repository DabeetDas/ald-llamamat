The performance of ellipsometry in characterising multilayer stacks has been benchmarked against the TEM measurements. We have used the impressive range of imaging and spectroscopic capabilities of the modern analytical TEM to understand the nature of the layers and the interfaces thereof, in our nanolaminates. Energy-ﬁltered TEM (EFTEM) was carried out to confirm the chemical distributions within the stacks. Unlike plasmon loss imaging, background effects can be removed using the three-window technique, and signal overlaps are not a common feature. Spectroscopic ellipsometry was carried out on both single-layer and multilayered stacks. Auger electron spectroscopy has been used to depth profile Al2O3/Ta2O5 multilayers.
