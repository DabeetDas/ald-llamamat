  "evidence": "HfO2 thin films were grown in 5, 10, and 20 cycles by ALD using Hf(NCH3)4 as Hf source and O3 with a concentration of 160 g/m3 as oxidant at 300 °C on 4 in. diameter p-type (100)Si substrates.
