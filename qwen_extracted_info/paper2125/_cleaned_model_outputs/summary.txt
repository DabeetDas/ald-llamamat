The island growth of Ru was observed at the initial stages of the film formation up to a nominal thickness of 11.1 nm. A maximum Ru nanocrystal spatial density of 9.7×1011/cm2 was achieved with an average size of 3.5 nm and standard deviation of the size of 20%.
