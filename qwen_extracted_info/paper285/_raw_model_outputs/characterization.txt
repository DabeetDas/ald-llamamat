The stoichiometry was determined using Rutherford backscattering spectrometry (RBS) with He+ ions at an energy of 1.4MeV. The absolute thickness of the films was measured with X-ray reflectometry (XRR) at an angle of incidence of 1◦ to 5◦. The variation of thickness over the sample area was observed with spectroscopic ellipsometry (SE) mapping at an angle of 70◦ in the wavelength range of 350 to 850 nm. Selected samples were also analysed by TEM in order to verify the thickness observations. The crystal structure of the as-deposited and thermally annealed films was determined by X-ray diffraction (XRD) using Cu Kα radiation. For the electrical characterization, capacitor stacks were prepared. The top contact was realized by depositing platinum using electron beam evaporation through a shadow mask. The electrical characterization of the capacitor stacks was performed on a measuring stage with precisely movable measuring tips. C–V-curves were recorded with an impedance analyser (HP 4192A) at a frequency of 100 kHz and a hold time of 3s per point. I–V-curves were recorded with a semiconductor parameter analyser (HP 4155B) with a hold time of30 s at each measuring point."
