  "evidence": "The film thicknesses of the deposited films were determined by measuring the optical reflectance spectrum (Hitachi U-2000 double-beam spectrophotometer) between wavelengths of 190 and 1100 nm and fitting a theoretical spectrum to the measured spectrum. In addition, a Bruker D8 Advance X-ray diffractometer, set in X-ray reflection (XRR) mode, was used to determine the film thicknesses and densities below 50 nm. Crystallite orientations and crystallinity of the deposited films were determined by X-ray diffraction (XRD) with Cu K radiation in a Philips MPD 1880 diffractometer. Surface morphology was studied with a Nanoscope III atomic force microscope (Digital Instruments) operated in tapping mode. Film compositions were measured by time-of-flight elastic recoil detection analysis (TOF-ERDA) at the Accelerator Laboratory of the University of Helsinki, Finland, and at IMEC, Belgium. For the electrical characterization, HfO2 films were deposited from Cp2Hf/CH3/O3 and Cp2Hf/CH3/H2O onto HF-etched or native SiO2-covered p-type Si(100) substrates at 350°C. The C-V characteristics were measured with an HP 4284A precision LCR meter. The I-V voltage curves were measured with a Keithley 2400."
