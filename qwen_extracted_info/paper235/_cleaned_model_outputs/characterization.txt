 "evidence": "X-ray diffraction analysis (XRD), ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy (UPS), XPS and spectro photometry were used to characterize the films. The surface morphologies and three-dimensional schemes were mapped with atomic force microscopy (NanoScope SPA300 system). The basic crystalline structural properties of the grown films were characterized by x-ray diffraction (Rigaku). The optical transmittance and optical absorption edge (JASCOV-560) and carrier transport properties of the as-grown films were also measured, along with the temperature dependence of conductivity, and Hall measurements were also performed. The method used for the thickness measurements is based on the technique of x-ray photoelectron spectroscopy depth profiling using signal intensities and argon ion sputtering, and etching time values were calibrated using both the stylus method and optical interference methods for tin dioxide films. The electronic density of states in the valence band of the sub-nanometre tin dioxide films was analysed by ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy (UPS) using a He-UV source (21.2 eV) and an Ulvac-Phi 1600 ultrahigh vacuum XPS system with an Mg Kα (1253.6 eV) x-ray source. Detecting the XPS signal during argon ion etching, the decrease of the tin signal intensities and oxygen intensities to their half values at the film interfaces and the growth of silicon and chemically shifted oxygen intensities from the glass substrates are observed after the same etching time. The carrier transport properties were measured in terms of resistivity and Hall mobility."
