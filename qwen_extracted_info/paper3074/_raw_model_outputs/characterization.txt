The growth behavior of ALD HfO2 was also monitored by Rutherford backscattering (RBS). Electrical measurements were made on planar n- and p-MOS devices, fabricated on both (100)- and (110)-orientated silicon substrates. Capacitance–voltage (C–V) characteristics were measured with an Agilent 4284A LCR meter at a frequency of 100 kHz. An estimation of the leakage current was done at a gate voltage of −1 V or V T − 0.6 V, on 20 devices with areas between 1 × 1 and 10 × 10 μm2. The channel conductance was determined with a Keithley 4200 tool at a drain voltage (Vd) of 40 mV.
