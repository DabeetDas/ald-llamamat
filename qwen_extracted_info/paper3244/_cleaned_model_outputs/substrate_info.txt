  "evidence": "The substrates employed during the ALD growth experiments were Si(100), with a 1.2 nm thick chemical SiO2 grown atop, and n-type In0.53Ga0.47As/InP which was initially capped with a 500 nm thick As layer. The In0.53Ga0.47As was decapped at 380 ◦C for 10 minutes in a molecular beam deposition system (Omicron) in ultra-high vacuum (UHV) environment. The resulting surface is atomically smooth, As-rich, and (2×4) reconstructed, as recently reported. Prior to loading into the ALD reactor, In0.53Ga0.47As substrates were cleaned with a 2 minutes long dip at room temperature in (NH4)2S solution (20%) in order to remove the native oxides formed in the transfer from the UHV environment and to impart As-S and Ga-S passivation bonding at the surface level."
