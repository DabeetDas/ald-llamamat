Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) analysis was carried out using an electrochemical workstation (CHI660D, CH Instruments) operated in a frequency range from 0.1 Hz to 100 kHz, while a potentiostat attached to the frequency response analyzer was employed under dark condition and at 10 mV (alternating current) amplitude."
