A constant growth rate of 0.23  (cycle)–1 was obtained on both substrates throughout the temperature range 250–350 C. In the case of the two Y(thd)3 adducts, the growth rate was found to be 0.23  (cycle)–1 for Y(thd)3(bipy), while for Y(thd)3(phen) it was slightly lower (0.22  (cycle)–1). Assuming that the dissociation of Y(thd)3(phen) to Y(thd)3 is not complete, the resulting (physically larger) chemisorbed molecule may lower the growth rate.
