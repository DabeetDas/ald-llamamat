Electrical measurements were performed at room temperature using an ECOPIA HMS-3000 Hall effect measurements system. Optical band gaps were calculated from transmittance and reflectance spectra, which were obtained using a PerkinElmer lambda 900 Spectrophotometer. The transmittance and the reflectance data of each film were measured for wavelengths in the range of 250–2500 nm."
